## SOS SMS go-to-market

Marketing copy, product strings, text message content, docs, and release notes in this repository are governed by the SOS SMS brain at `../SOSSMSGTMBrain`. Read `../SOSSMSGTMBrain/AGENTS.md`, then `rules/do-not-say.md` and `rules/feature-status.md`, before suggesting any contractor-facing or customer-facing text.

Four rules override any suggestion you would otherwise make:

1. Nothing is shipped. A capability is never written in the present tense as if it were live.
2. A number, price, arrival time, name, or quote that is not in the brain is never written at all. {TBD} and [brackets] stay.
3. The name is always "SOS SMS", and the safety line goes on any message inviting an urgent text.
4. Every draft passes `../SOSSMSGTMBrain/ops/QA-checklist.md` before it is offered.

Sides, never blended:
- Contractors (supply): no charge to see a request, and the trip paid before you roll. Cold email, partners, referral loop.
- Customers (demand): one text, an arrival time on acceptance, a 90-minute window with money back when missed, [scope] pending. Search ads, problem pages, product loops.
