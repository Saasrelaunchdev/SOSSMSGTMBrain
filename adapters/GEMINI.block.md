## SOS SMS go-to-market

The SOS SMS go-to-market is held as files at `../SOSSMSGTMBrain`. Load `../SOSSMSGTMBrain/AGENTS.md` and the four ledgers it names (`rules/glossary.md`, `rules/feature-status.md`, `rules/do-not-say.md`, `rules/writing-rules.md`) before writing anything a contractor or member will see.

Pick the side first, never blend them:
- Contractors (supply): no charge to see a request, and the trip paid before you roll. Cold email, partners, referral loop.
- Members (demand): homeowners who join a monthly or annual membership before the need, with the card and address on file. One text, and help is on the way: an arrival time on acceptance, and a 90-minute window from the request text with the $99 trip charge back when missed, in the zone's covered areas and hours. Affiliates and email before the need, search and SEO pages for sign-ups, member loops.

Non-negotiables:
- Pre-launch: nothing is live; nothing in the present tense as if it were.
- No invented numbers, prices, arrival times, counts, or testimonials. Every value comes from `reference/resolution-register.md`; per-message details and the membership prices are {{merge fields}}.
- Always "SOS SMS", never the first word alone. SOS SMS dispatches; never the plumber, a warranty, insurance, an emergency service, or 911.
- The safety line on everything that describes the emergency text: "For fire, a gas smell, or anyone hurt, call 911 first."
- "Request", never "lead". Never "escrow", "exclusive", "pre-qualified", "guaranteed arrival time".
- No em or en dashes. Banned words: leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, streamline, cutting-edge, empower.

Run `../SOSSMSGTMBrain/ops/QA-checklist.md` before returning a draft. New market input goes to `../SOSSMSGTMBrain/ops/signal-log.md`, not into the copy.
