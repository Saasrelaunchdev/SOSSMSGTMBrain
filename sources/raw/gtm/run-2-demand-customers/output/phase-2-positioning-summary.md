# Positioning summary, customer side

SOS SMS can lead the customer side of its category on two vectors: a guaranteed arrival window, and many contractors reached with one reply. The first answers the most common complaint about emergency plumbing, the no-show, with a promise that costs SOS SMS money when it is broken. The second removes both failures of the alternatives at once: the customer does not call down a list at 2 a.m., and does not get flooded by every business that saw the request. Acquisition follows from saying exactly that in the moment of need and, more cheaply, before it: a number saved in a phone before the pipe bursts is worth more than an ad seen during the flood.

Growth is bounded by the contractor side. The guarantee is only honest where Run 1 has reached the density number, so the customer side expands zone by zone behind supply, never ahead of it. That same constraint is the defense: a competitor cannot offer a money-backed arrival window in a zone without first building the contractor density SOS SMS already has there. The positions SOS SMS should not contest are brand familiarity, where national brands lead, and guarantees on the work itself, where the marketplaces lead. Unless the founders decide to stand behind workmanship, SOS SMS says plainly that it checks licenses and insurance, not the quality of the repair.
