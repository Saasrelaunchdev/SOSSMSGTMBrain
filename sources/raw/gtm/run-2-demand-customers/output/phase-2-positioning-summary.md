# Positioning summary, member side

SOS SMS can lead the member side of its category on two vectors: the emergency as one text with a money-backed arrival window, and many contractors reached with one reply. Every plan a household can buy today leaves the emergency itself alone: warranties and utility plans pay for the repair after a claim and a wait, a plumber's club gives priority with one company, and doing nothing means searching and calling at the worst moment. Membership is what makes the difference possible: the household joins before anything breaks, with the card and address on file, so a number saved in a phone before the pipe bursts is worth more than an ad seen during the flood, and acquisition happens where homeowners already trust someone, through partners and email, long before the need.

Growth is bounded by the contractor side. The guarantee is only honest where Run 1 has reached the density number, so memberships open zone by zone behind supply, never ahead of it. That same constraint is the defense: a competitor cannot offer a one-text, money-backed arrival window in a zone without first building the contractor density SOS SMS already has there. The positions SOS SMS should not contest are repair coverage, where warranties and utility plans lead, and brand familiarity, where national plumbers lead. SOS SMS does not pay for or guarantee the repair (R21), and it says plainly that it checks licenses and insurance, not the quality of the repair.
