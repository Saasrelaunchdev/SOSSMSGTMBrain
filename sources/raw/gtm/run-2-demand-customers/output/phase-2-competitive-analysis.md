**Scoring basis.** V1: payment for covered repairs (S78, S79); plumber clubs discount repairs (S75); SOS SMS pays for none (R21). V2: brand recognition at the door (S40, S67, S68, S93); SOS SMS is unknown and open only where density is met. V3: how the emergency itself starts and whether lateness costs the provider (S20 disclaims timely service; S40 pays for lateness on appointments; S75 gives priority with one company; SOS SMS starts dispatch with one text and refunds the trip charge on a miss). V4: how many providers one request reaches, against how many contact the homeowner back (S46, S48).

**The scenario this creates.** SOS SMS is the obvious choice for one segment at this stage: homeowners inside a dense founding zone who want the emergency itself to be one text, with a committed arrival time and money back if it is missed. It is not the choice for a household that wants its repairs paid for (D3, D4) or one brand it already trusts at the door (D2). Many members will hold a warranty or a utility plan as well: those pay for the repair, SOS SMS gets someone there. The plumber club is the closest rival on V3, and the difference is reach: one company's priority against many contractors at once, with a window from the text.

## Step 2.15. Category summary

**Vector 1: Pays for repairs**
Leader: D3 home warranties, then D4 utility-partner plans.
Impact: a plan that pays for the repair sells against the fear of a big bill. SOS SMS does not compete here: it gets someone to the door and does not pay for the work (R21). Many members will hold both.

**Vector 2: A known brand at the door**
Leader: D2 plumber clubs.
Impact: a name the household already knows wins the decision to join without persuasion. SOS SMS is new, and it says so.

**Vector 3: One text, money-backed arrival window**
Leader: SOS SMS, projected (9.0), with D2 plumber clubs next for priority with one company.
Impact: answers the moment every plan leaves alone, the emergency itself: no search, no claim, no form, and a promise that costs SOS SMS money when it is broken.

**Vector 4: Many contractors reached, one reply**
Leader: SOS SMS, projected (9.0).
Impact: removes both failures of the alternatives at once: calling down a list until someone answers, and being flooded by everyone who did. It is the hardest thing for a single company's club or a quote marketplace to copy.
