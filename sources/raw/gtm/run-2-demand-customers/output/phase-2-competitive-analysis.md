**Scoring basis.** V1: 24/7 availability and brand recognition (S41, S43, S49, S68). SOS SMS is unknown and its availability depends on density, so its projection is low. V2: money-back or satisfaction guarantees on the work (S44, S45); Google's consumer guarantee ended in 2025 (S09); Roto-Rooter's and Benjamin Franklin's guarantees on the work itself were not researched, so their scores are estimates. SOS SMS checks licenses, not workmanship. V3: a money-backed arrival promise (S40 for scheduled appointments; S20 disclaims one). V4: how many providers one request reaches, against how many contact the customer back (S46, S48).

**The scenario this creates.** SOS SMS is the obvious choice for one segment at this stage: homeowners inside a dense founding zone who have no contractor answering at night and want to send one message and get one committed arrival time. It is not the choice for someone who wants a known brand (D2, D3) or a guarantee on the work itself (D4, D5). Benjamin Franklin is the closest rival on V3, and the difference is that its guarantee runs against a scheduled appointment while SOS SMS's runs against an emergency window.

## Step 2.15. Category summary

**Vector 1: Always-open, trusted brand**
Leader: D2 Roto-Rooter.
Impact: a name people already know at 2 a.m. wins the first call without any persuasion. Acquisition runs on brand and search position; the cost shows up later as price trust.

**Vector 2: Guarantee on the work itself**
Leader: D4 Angi, then D5 Thumbtack.
Impact: the marketplaces use guarantees to make strangers safe to hire, which converts quote requests into bookings. SOS SMS does not compete here: its guarantee covers arrival, not the repair (decided, R21).

**Vector 3: Guaranteed arrival window**
Leader: SOS SMS, projected (8.8), with D3 Benjamin Franklin close behind for scheduled work.
Impact: answers the first complaint in emergency plumbing, the no-show. A promise that costs SOS SMS money when broken is credible in a way "fast response" is not.

**Vector 4: Many contractors reached, one reply**
Leader: SOS SMS, projected (9.0).
Impact: removes both failures of the alternatives at once: calling down a list until someone answers, and being flooded by everyone who did. It is the most specific thing SOS SMS can say, and the hardest for a single company or a quote marketplace to copy.
