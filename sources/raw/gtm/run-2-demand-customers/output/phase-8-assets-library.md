# Phase 8. Assets and Collaterals Library Dashboard, member side

Revised 2026-10-11, the membership revision, and 2026-10-20, the tiers revision. Homeowners join before the need, so SOS SMS meets them where they already trust someone, through partners and email, long before anything breaks. Search pages and paid social sell sign-ups in open zones, never dispatch during an emergency; there is no paid search at launch (decision 11). One membership covers plumbing, HVAC, and electrical, on three plans: Basic, Plus, and Premier. No sales team and no calls to households: partners join an affiliate program, and the product does the converting.

| LinkedIn outreach | 3. Search pages and paid social, for sign-ups | Prevention and problem guides for plumbing, HVAC, and electrical, read before the need and found in organic search, the membership page, and zone pages; paid social for sign-ups, limited to open zones' maps, with no retargeting and no pixels. No paid search at launch |

Plus the **member loops**: the saved contact card, Text READY, the forwardable card, the two questions after a job, upgrades, and renewal.

**What was dropped, and why.** Paid search at launch: at plumbing benchmarks a new paid member costs about $137, about 3 times a Basic member's ceiling, and the searches with volume come from people mid-emergency, who are better served by calling a contractor from the results than by joining anything (decision 11, revised 2026-10-20). Also out: ads that sell dispatch during an emergency; sales calls, demos, meetings, and door-to-door; marketing texts without written consent (S31); retargeting and ad pixels; Local Services Ads (S70); payments to anyone referring at a home purchase settlement (RESPA, S86, S94); LinkedIn.

| The ceiling | Paid social scales only while its measured cost per new paid member stays at or under the ceiling of the plan joined (decision 12) |

### Measurement

The conversion to optimize for is a **new paid member**, by plan, not a click, a ZIP check, or a launch list sign-up; those are tracked as secondary conversions. Cost per new paid member by channel, plan, and zone, with affiliate commissions counted in it, is measured against the ceiling of the plan joined, about $47 Basic, $70 Plus, and $151 Premier (R63): every channel scales while it is at or under the ceiling and stops when it is not. Monthly churn, annual renewal, and upgrades by channel show which channels bring members who stay (`../../marketplace/speed-to-scale-plan.md`, sections 5 and 8).

## Production order

1. Stage 0 first: the member terms and the automatic renewal disclosures reviewed by counsel (S87); membership billing for the three plans tested, with upgrades and the late-arrival credit; the affiliate terms (disclosure, S88; no settlement-service payments, S86, S94); email sending set up (S73); the number registered for A2P 10DLC (decision 9, S32). Without them, nothing can sell a membership.
2. The membership page with the three plans, the guarantee in full, and how it works in one text. They carry decisions 2, 3, 4, and 6.
3. During Stage 1, while supply fills the zone in all three trades: the readiness scorecard, the first five prevention and problem guides, and the launch list sequence.
4. The affiliate program page and the partner kit; the first partners signed for the launch zone, with codes ready before it opens.
5. Member loops: the welcome texts, the first-steps reply, the reply to a non-member, the contact card, the credit and no-show text, READY.
6. When the zone opens: the opening email to the launch list; affiliates go live; then paid social for sign-ups. No paid search at launch (decision 11).

## Name collision note

A web search for "SOS SMS" returns several unrelated local plumbing companies with "SOS" in their names (decision 10: a US trademark search and a search check in the zone, in Stage 0). Should a brand search campaign ever run (R68), this has a direct cost: brand searches for "SOS" may belong to someone else, and an ad that reads like theirs invites confusion. Use the full name in every ad, email, and page, and make the saved contact card, not the name, the way members reach SOS SMS.
