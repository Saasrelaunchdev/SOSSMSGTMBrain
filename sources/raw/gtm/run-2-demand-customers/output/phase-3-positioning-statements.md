# Phase 3. Positioning Dashboard, member side

Three statements in the skill's template, one against each way a household gets ready today, each with the conditions that must be true before it is used outside the company. Revised 2026-10-11, the membership revision: the statements sell a membership joined before the need, and the guarantee runs from the member's request text (decision 3).

**Safety line, required wherever a statement invites an urgent text:** for fire, a gas smell, or anyone hurt, call 911 first. If you smell gas, leave the building before you call (S60).

## Step 3.1. Positioning statements

**Against doing nothing in advance (search and call when it happens):**
Not "one more number to try at 2 a.m.", the number you save before anything breaks. As a member, the emergency is one text: it reaches the licensed, insured contractors on SOS SMS for that trade near your home at once, the first who can come commits to an arrival time, and if they are not there within 90 minutes of your text, the $99 trip charge comes back.

- Must be true before use: memberships are open in the zone, which means the density number is met for the trade and the covered hours are named (Run 1 Phase 5, R55); the guarantee scope is stated as decided (decision 3) in the same place.
- The honest cost, named first when asked: a membership costs something every month or year whether or not anything breaks (R46). The answer is what it includes, the trip charge only on use, and cancel any time (R53, R56).

**Against plumber club memberships (Benjamin Franklin, Roto-Rooter):**
Not "priority with one company", every vetted contractor on SOS SMS for the trade near you, at once. A plumber's club puts you first in one company's queue. One text to SOS SMS reaches every licensed, insured contractor on SOS SMS for that trade nearby, and whoever can come first commits to an arrival time inside a 90-minute window from your text.

- Must be true before use: memberships are open in the zone (R55), so more than one contractor is on SOS SMS for the trade at the hours named.
- Benjamin Franklin's club gives a reduced trip charge, 10 percent off repairs, and priority scheduling (S75), and its punctuality guarantee pays for lateness on scheduled appointments (S40). The difference to state, when asked: SOS SMS's window runs from an emergency text, not from a booked appointment, and SOS SMS gives no discount on the repair.

**Against home warranties and utility-partner plans (American Home Shield, HomeServe):**
Not "coverage", someone at your door. A warranty or a repair plan pays for a covered repair after a claim. SOS SMS gets a vetted contractor to you with one text, gives you an arrival time when one accepts, and shares your address only with the contractor who takes the job.

- Must be true before use: the data rule (brief decision 6) is in the privacy policy and the member terms, in the same words.
- For a covered failure, the warranty or the plan is usually the cheaper route for the repair. Say so if asked: SOS SMS does not pay for repairs (R21), and many members will hold both.
- AHS's contract allows 48 hours on weekends to contact a contractor and does not guarantee timely emergency service (S20); the HomeServe plan sold through CenterPoint carries a 30-day waiting period (S78). Internal comparison; cite only after verifying the current contract.

## Positioning in one line

For homeowners who want to be ready before something breaks, SOS SMS is the membership that makes a home emergency one text: the vetted contractors nearby see it at once, the first who can come commits to an arrival time, and if they miss the 90-minute window the trip charge comes back.

## What this positioning deliberately does not claim

| Do not claim | Why |
|---|---|
| "Every contractor in your area" | Only contractors on SOS SMS are reached (brief Section 5) |
| Any average or typical arrival time | None exists; pre-launch |
| That SOS SMS prevents damage or saves money | No evidence; the guarantee covers arrival, not outcome |
| That the membership pays for repairs, or any discount on them | Not offered (R21, R53); SOS SMS is not a home warranty or insurance |
| A guarantee on the repair itself | Not offered (decided, R21); SOS SMS checks licenses and insurance, not workmanship |
| "Covered everywhere" or "24/7" without the hours | Memberships open zone by zone at the density number, with covered hours named (R55) |
| "Emergency service" or anything that reads as 911 | The name invites it; the safety line is required instead |
| "Never shared" | The accepting contractor must receive the address and details |
