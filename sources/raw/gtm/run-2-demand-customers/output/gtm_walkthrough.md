# SLIDE 1, SOS SMS Member GTM: What the Dashboard Suite Is For

- SOS SMS is a pre-launch, two-sided network for emergency home repair, and this deck covers the member side
- Nine dashboards plus a lead magnet answer one question: why would a homeowner join SOS SMS before anything breaks?
- The answer the suite builds toward: join once, on Basic, Plus, or Premier, and a plumbing, HVAC, or electrical emergency is one text that reaches the vetted contractors nearby; one accepts with an arrival time, and on Plus and Premier a missed 90-minute window costs SOS SMS money
- Every SOS SMS metric is a planning value until the zone runs, and no arrival time is claimed
- So what: memberships open only in zones where the contractor side has reached density in all three trades

# SLIDE 2, Product Feature Matrix Dashboard: What We Learned and What We Lead With

- The lead: one text starts dispatch, because the card and address went on file at joining (item 14)
- Hero features with it: one text that reaches the network in plumbing, HVAC, and electrical, the arrival time on acceptance, the 90-minute guarantee on Plus and Premier, privacy in its narrow form, and the readiness kit that gives value on day one
- The membership itself is table stakes: households already know the format from clubs, warranties, and utility plans, so it opens the door and never leads
- A price range before arrival is wanted by all and offered by few; it is not offered at launch (R49)
- So what: lead with one text and the arrival time; the plan prices and the $99 trip charge on use are stated before anyone joins

# SLIDE 3, Competitive Market Analysis Dashboard: Competitor Map and Buying Context

- Six ways a household gets ready today: nothing in advance, plumber and HVAC clubs, home warranties, utility-partner plans, Frontdoor, and on-demand marketplaces
- Members-to-be praise priority, covered repairs, and plans that arrive through a trusted utility
- They complain about unanswered calls at night, slow claims, waiting periods, upselling, and floods of calls from quote sites
- SOS SMS does not pay for repairs and checks licenses and insurance, not workmanship, and says so
- So what: compete on the emergency itself, one text for three trades with a guaranteed window on Plus and Premier, not on repair coverage or brand

# SLIDE 4, Positioning Dashboard: Four Vectors, Current State and Projected State

- They lead: paying for repairs (warranties, utility-partner plans) and a known brand at the door (plumber clubs)
- We lead, projected: one text with an arrival window guaranteed on Plus and Premier (9.0 of 10) and many contractors reached with one reply (9.0 of 10)
- Current state: 0 on every vector, because nothing is live
- Positioning line: the membership that makes a plumbing, HVAC, or electrical emergency one text, with 6 months of membership credited on Plus, and 12 on Premier, if the window is missed
- So what: the guarantee is only honest where density is met, which makes density the moat as well as the constraint

# SLIDE 5, ICP Dashboard: Who We Win First, and Why

- Homeowners in open zones, in two groups: new homeowners without a go-to contractor, and established homeowners whose contractor does not answer at night
- Both join before the need, through the partners and lists that already reach them, never through an ad at the moment of the emergency
- Triggers: moving in, the first freeze or heat wave, a neighbor's story, a night when nobody answered
- Renters and property managers are outside this run
- So what: grow each zone's launch list while supply fills it, then open with email and affiliates

# SLIDE 6, Buying Committee Dashboard: Personas, Blockers, Drivers

- A household: one or two adults decide, over days, before anything breaks
- Blockers: another subscription, "it rarely happens", whether the home is covered at night, a card on file with a new company
- Drivers: feeling ready, someone to reach at night, the cost known in advance, cancel any time
- The launch zone's member area is the neighborhoods inside the launch zone
- So what: the membership page has to answer every blocker before it asks for the card

---

# SLIDE 7, Customer Journey Funnel Dashboard: Stage Progression and Internal KPIs

- Two clocks: weeks before the need, from awareness to joining, and minutes during it
- Evaluation happens on the membership page: the three plans, the trip charge on use, the guarantee with its plans and scope, what is not included, cancel any time
- KPIs: cost per new paid member by channel and plan, time to acceptance by trade, share arriving within the window, late arrivals and no-shows, monthly churn, annual renewal
- Blockers: another subscription, coverage at night, carrier registration, the no-acceptance case, a card that fails
- So what: credits applied automatically, and a membership that stays worth keeping in the months nothing breaks

- Seven questions on the shutoff, the water heater, the home's age, and who to call at night in three trades

# SLIDE 9, Assets and Collaterals Library Dashboard: Channel Coverage and TOFU to BOFU Flow

- Pre-need channels: affiliates and email lead; search pages and paid social support sign-ups in open zones; no paid search at launch
- Affiliates carry a code and a disclosed commission; nobody is paid for referrals at a home purchase settlement
- Bottom of funnel: the membership page and the guarantee in full; then the welcome texts and the first-steps reply
- Ads sell memberships before the need, never dispatch during an emergency; Local Services Ads are closed to this model
- So what: every asset targets open zones, and the launch zone first

# SLIDE 10, Pricing and Packaging Dashboard: Value Metric, Usage Metric, Tiers, Expansion Levers

- Value metric: per household, on three plans billed monthly or annually; no fee per request
- Usage metric: requests per member per year by trade, planning value 0.50 in all, and members per zone against about 410
- Packaging: Basic $10 a month or $100 a year, Plus $15 or $150 with the guarantee and up to 5 phones, Premier $35 or $350 with a 12-month credit, a trip charge a year, a second home, and up to 10 phones; Plus on the annual plan shown first; plus the $99 trip charge on use, credited to the repair
- About $9.29, $13.77, and $29.62 a month of contribution set acquisition ceilings of about $47, $70, and $151: affiliates fit under every one, paid search at plumbing benchmarks does not, so there is none at launch
- Expansion levers: upgrades to Plus and Premier, more trades behind the same membership, more zones at density, annual plans, and member referral
- So what: two prices, both stated before joining, and nothing to agree to at the worst moment
