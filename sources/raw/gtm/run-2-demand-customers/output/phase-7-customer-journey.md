# Phase 7. Customer Journey Funnel Dashboard, member side

The member's journey runs on two clocks: weeks before the need, from awareness to joining, and minutes during it, once something breaks. Revised 2026-10-11, the membership revision: homeowners arrive through affiliates, email, guides, and search for sign-ups, and join before the need; nobody from SOS SMS calls them. Per `../SKILL-ADAPTATION.md`, Stage 7 reads as the skill assumes: a recurring membership that renews or does not.

At launch the founders cover every function in the last column; "Product" means the product does it without a person.

## Step 7.2. Key journey insights

<!-- sheet: Insights -->
| Category | Insight |
|---|---|
| Critical Success Factor | Supply before memberships. Memberships open only in zones and hours where Run 1's density number is met (R55); everywhere else, the launch list |
| Critical Success Factor | The membership page decides. It states the price, the trip charge on use, the guarantee with its scope, what is not included, and how to cancel, before the card is taken |
| Critical Success Factor | The first reply to a request text decides the emergency: at once, that it is going to the contractors now, the safety line, and how to reach a person. No offer, no link, nothing to confirm |
| Critical Success Factor | Refunds are paid automatically and promptly, without the member asking. A guarantee that has to be claimed is a guarantee nobody believes |
| Critical Success Factor | Retention. Most months nothing breaks, so the membership has to stay worth keeping: the readiness kit, seasonal notes, and a renewal reminder that says plainly what it renews (R56, R59) |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **Another subscription.** A fee every month or year before anything breaks (R46). State what membership includes, the trip charge only on use, and cancel any time, before the card |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **Is my home covered at night.** Memberships open per zone at the density number, for its covered hours. The membership page names both, and the ZIP check says plainly when a home is outside |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **Can the text get through.** Business texting from local numbers needs carrier registration; unregistered traffic has been blocked since February 2025 (S32). Silence reads as "nobody is there" |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **What if no one accepts.** What the member is told, and when, is the moment the guarantee is tested. Decided (decision 3): told within 15 minutes, with stop-the-water steps, and nothing charged |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **A card that fails.** The card on file is billed monthly or annually, so a failed card shows up at billing, before an emergency finds it, and the member is told by email |
| Acceleration Opportunity | Affiliates who already reach homeowners: a utility's new-account offer, an association's welcome packet, a newsletter's feature. One partner can reach a whole zone at once, the model behind HomeServe's more than 1,300 partners (S93) |
| Acceleration Opportunity | The launch list. Every scorecard and guide sign-up in a zone not yet open is a member waiting for the opening email (Stage 2) |
| Acceleration Opportunity | Founding contractors' own customers: a founding contractor who does not work nights can point their customers to SOS SMS membership for after-hours cover, keeping daytime work for themselves |
| Acceleration Opportunity | The first freeze concentrates intent. Run the prevention guides and the readiness scorecard before it, and open sign-ups only where contractor density holds |
| Acceleration Opportunity | Microsoft Ads for sign-ups: clicks reported 30 to 60 percent cheaper, with an older homeowner audience (S74, C). Import the Google campaigns in every open zone |
