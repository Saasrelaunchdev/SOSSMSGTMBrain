# Phase 7. Customer Journey Funnel Dashboard, customer side

The customer's journey compresses into minutes once something breaks. Revised 2026-10-01 for the product-led motion: customers arrive through bottom-of-funnel search ads, problem-solving pages, or the product loops, and nobody from SOS SMS contacts them first. Per `../SKILL-ADAPTATION.md`, Stage 7 reads as the saved number and referral, because emergencies are rare for any one household.

At launch the founders cover every function in the last column; "Product" means the product does it without a person.

## Step 7.2. Key journey insights

<!-- sheet: Insights -->
| Category | Insight |
|---|---|
| Critical Success Factor | The first reply decides everything. It must say who SOS SMS is, what happens next, the trip charge, and the guarantee scope before any payment link is sent |
| Critical Success Factor | Density before demand. The guarantee is sold only in zones and hours where Run 1's target is met |
| Critical Success Factor | Refunds are paid automatically and promptly, without the customer asking. A guarantee that has to be claimed is a guarantee nobody believes |
| Critical Success Factor | The owned share of requests. Paid search buys the first request from a household; the saved number and shares have to bring the next ones (speed-to-scale plan, section 5) |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **Can the buyer pay.** The trip charge is paid at night through a link in a text. No card at hand, or a declined card, ends the job before dispatch. 4.2 percent of households are unbanked (S61); decide what happens for them |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **Does the link look like a scam.** A payment link by text from an unknown number is the pattern of the most-reported text scams, fake delivery and unpaid toll texts; consumers reported losing $470 million to text-initiated scams in 2024 (S59). Earn trust in the first reply, before the link; never send a link first |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **Can the text get through.** Business texting from local numbers needs carrier registration; unregistered traffic has been blocked since February 2025 (S32). Silence reads as "nobody is there" |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **What if no one accepts.** What the customer is told, and when, is the moment the guarantee is tested. It needs a written answer (open decision 4) before launch |
| Acceleration Opportunity | Problem keywords ("water heater leaking from bottom") often cost less than "emergency plumber near me" and land on pages that already help; build them first |
| Acceleration Opportunity | Founding contractors' own customers: a founding contractor who does not work nights can give SOS SMS's number as their after-hours line, keeping daytime work for themselves |
| Acceleration Opportunity | Microsoft Ads: clicks reported 30 to 60 percent cheaper, with an older homeowner audience (S74, C). Import the Google campaigns in every zone |
| Acceleration Opportunity | The first freeze concentrates demand. Raise bids only where contractor density holds, and run the prevention campaign before it, not during |
