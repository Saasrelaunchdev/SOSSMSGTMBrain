# Phase 7. Customer Journey Funnel Dashboard, member side

The member's journey runs on two clocks: weeks before the need, from awareness to joining, and minutes during it, once something breaks. Revised 2026-10-11, the membership revision: homeowners arrive through affiliates, email, guides, and paid social for sign-ups, and join before the need, on one of three plans (revised 2026-10-20; no paid search at launch); nobody from SOS SMS calls them. Per `../SKILL-ADAPTATION.md`, Stage 7 reads as the skill assumes: a recurring membership that renews or does not.

At launch the founders cover every function in the last column; "Product" means the product does it without a person.

## Step 7.2. Key journey insights

<!-- sheet: Insights -->
| Category | Insight |
|---|---|
| Critical Success Factor | Supply before memberships. Memberships open only in zones and hours where Run 1's density number is met (R55); everywhere else, the launch list |
| Critical Success Factor | The membership page decides. It states the three plans and their prices, the trip charge on use, the guarantee with its plans and scope, what is not included, and how to cancel, before the card is taken |
| Critical Success Factor | The first reply to a request text decides the emergency: at once, that it is going to the contractors now, the safety line, and how to reach a person. No offer, no link, nothing to confirm |
| Critical Success Factor | Credits and no-show refunds are applied automatically and promptly, without the member asking. A guarantee that has to be claimed is a guarantee nobody believes |
| Critical Success Factor | Retention. Most months nothing breaks, so the membership has to stay worth keeping: the readiness kit, seasonal notes, and a renewal reminder that says plainly what it renews (R56, R59) |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **Another subscription.** A fee every month or year before anything breaks (R46). State what membership includes, the trip charge only on use, and cancel any time, before the card |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **Is my home covered at night.** Yes, in an open zone: memberships open per zone only when every hour band meets the density number (R15, R55). The membership page names the neighborhoods, and the ZIP check says plainly when a home is outside |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **Can the text get through.** Business texting from local numbers needs carrier registration; unregistered traffic has been blocked since February 2025 (S32). Silence reads as "nobody is there" |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **What if no one accepts.** What the member is told, and when, is the moment the guarantee is tested. Decided (decision 3): told within 15 minutes, with the first steps, and nothing charged |
| Primary Journey Blocker | **A card that fails.** The card on file is billed monthly or annually, so a failed card shows up at billing, before an emergency finds it, and the member is told by email |
| Acceleration Opportunity | Affiliates who already reach homeowners: a utility's new-account offer, an association's welcome packet, a newsletter's feature. One partner can reach a whole zone at once, the model behind HomeServe's more than 1,300 partners (S93) |
| Acceleration Opportunity | The launch list. Every scorecard and guide sign-up in a zone not yet open is a member waiting for the opening email (Stage 2) |
| Acceleration Opportunity | Contractors' own customers: a contractor who does not work nights can refer their customers to SOS SMS membership through the affiliate program, on the same terms as any partner (R61), knowing a member's request goes to every matched contractor at once |
| Acceleration Opportunity | The first freeze and the first heat wave concentrate intent. Run the prevention guides and the readiness scorecard before it, and open sign-ups only where contractor density holds |
| Acceleration Opportunity | Upgrades from Basic to Plus: a member who sees the arrival time work once is the easiest Plus member to win. The upgrade note after a first job, and at renewal (R54) |
