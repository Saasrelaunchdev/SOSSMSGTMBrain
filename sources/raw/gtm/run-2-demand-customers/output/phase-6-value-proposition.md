# Phase 6. Value Proposition Dashboard, customer side

Three pillars, nine reasons to believe, nine copy drills. Written to `../GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 5 and checked against Section 6.

**The fabrication rule for this phase.** SOS SMS has no customers, jobs, or arrival times on record. Every "Headline (Social Proof)" is a method line that describes how the process works, and every "Supporting Proof Point" names the record or document that will show the claim is true from the first job. A real result replaces a method line only once it is measured and permissioned. No drill states or implies an average arrival time, a count of homes helped, or that SOS SMS prevents damage.

**Decided terms.** Every line that waited on a decision carries the decided words (`../../marketplace/resolution-register.md`, decided 2026-10-03). Customer copy goes out only once its zone opens; per-zone details are merge fields filled from the zone record.

**Safety line.** Every drill whose call to action invites a text carries: "For fire, a gas smell, or anyone hurt, call 911 first." Gas utilities tell people to leave the building before calling and not to use phones near a leak (S60), so for gas the line becomes: "Smell gas? Get out first, then call 911 and your gas company."

## Proof capture plan

1. The terms of service carry the permission clause before the first customer (Phase 5).
2. After every completed job, one text: "Did your contractor arrive within the window? Would you tell a neighbor?" Answers recorded verbatim with the date.
3. Arrival times are logged from acceptance to arrival, so that a real figure, with its sample size and date, can be published once it exists, and only if it holds up.
