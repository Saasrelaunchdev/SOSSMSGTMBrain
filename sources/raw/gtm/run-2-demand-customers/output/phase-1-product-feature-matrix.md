## Step 1.6. Demand driver

Revised 2026-10-11 for the membership: the demand driver works before the need, where affiliates and email reach homeowners, and it ends in joining.

Rule check: immediate value, and a problem SOS SMS is built to solve. The narrowed form of "only SOS SMS": a membership whose use is one text that reaches the network for the trade, with an arrival time and a guarantee.

<!-- sheet: Demand_Drivers -->
| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| What it is | **The readiness check, before anything breaks.** "How Ready Is Your Home for a 2 a.m. Leak?": seven questions, a score, and a plan, on the web or by texting READY. Affiliates and emails carry it; it ends with joining in an open zone, the launch list in a zone not yet open, and the plan alone outside |
| Immediate value | The household learns where its main shutoff is and whether it turns, the two things that keep a leak from spreading. EPA guidance is to dry water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold (S58), so the first hours matter |
| How it helps in problem identification | The questions surface the gap a homeowner rarely sees until it is too late: no one who comes at night, and no plan for the first ten minutes |
| How it exposes the need for SOS SMS | A low score on "who would come at 2 a.m." is answered on the same page: join now, and the next emergency is one text |
| ICP fit | Both levels. Level 1 has no one to call; Level 2's plumber does not answer at night |
| Conversion lever | Value before the ask: the plan is useful whether or not they join, and joining takes about 3 minutes with the card and address on file (R54) |
| Top-of-funnel use case | Every affiliate kit, partner email, newsletter blurb, and prevention guide links to it; Text READY is printed on every card |

Limits: the steps cover shutoff and safety only, never repairs, and are reviewed by a licensed plumber. No damage-cost figure without a source; no claim that SOS SMS prevents damage. Gas, fire, or injury get the 911 line first (S60).
