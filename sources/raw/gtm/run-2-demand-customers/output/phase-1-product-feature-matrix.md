## Step 1.6. Demand driver

Revised 2026-10-01 for the product-led motion: the demand driver lives inside the product, at the moment of need, where paid search and problem pages deliver customers.

Rule check: immediate value, and a problem SOS SMS is built to solve. The narrowed form of "only SOS SMS": one text that reaches the network for the trade, with an arrival time and a guarantee.

<!-- sheet: Demand_Drivers -->
| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| What it is | **Stop-the-water help, in the first reply.** Whatever brings a customer in (a search ad, a problem page, a saved number), their first text gets an automated reply with steps to stop the water for the problem they described, a safety check, and then the offer of a licensed plumber, with the trip charge stated |
| Immediate value | Shutting off the water is what keeps a leak from spreading while help is on the way. EPA guidance is to dry water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold (S58), so the first hours matter |
| How it helps in problem identification | The steps tell the customer whether they can wait (the water is off, the leak stopped) or need someone tonight |
| How it exposes the need for SOS SMS | When the water is off but the pipe is still broken, the next line in the same thread is the answer: a vetted plumber with an arrival time, one reply away |
| ICP fit | Both levels. Level 1 has no one to call; Level 2's own plumber is not answering |
| Conversion lever | Value before payment, in the same thread. The customer has already been helped by SOS SMS before being asked for anything |
| Top-of-funnel use case | Every search ad and problem page ends in the text button that starts this reply; every saved contact card leads to it |

Limits: the steps cover shutoff and safety only, never repairs, and are reviewed by a licensed plumber. Gas, fire, or injury get the 911 line first (S60).
