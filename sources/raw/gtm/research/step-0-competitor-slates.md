### Selection rule applied

A competitor earns a slot only if a contractor actually weighs it against SOS SMS. Applied:

- **Kept:** Angi Leads, Thumbtack, and Google Local Services Ads, because they are the three per-lead options a contractor in any US metro has already been pitched, and the ones "no lead fees" is compared against.
- **Kept:** Service Direct, as the strongest emergency-specific analog: exclusive calls, water damage focus, published prices. It is also the direct counter to SOS SMS's broadcast dispatch.
- **Kept:** word of mouth plus the contractor's own free listing, combined as one "status quo" column. The brief predicted it would be the strongest rival for owner-operators, and the research supports that it is where most homeowners start (S26, S27).
- **Kept as a sixth column:** home warranty and insurer networks, represented by American Home Shield's network, because they compete directly for the same after-hours contractor capacity and set the bar for "free to join, but you take our price".
- **Profiled but not a column:** answering services and dispatchers (slot 5). They are a cost, not a source of jobs, so they appear in Phase 3 (against indirect alternatives) and in Phase 9 (Anchor 2).
- **Dropped:** Jiffy and Puls. Jiffy's US footprint is two metros and its customer TrustScore is 1.4 (S16). Puls is mostly appliance repair. Neither is a likely comparison for a plumbing contractor in an unnamed metro. Revisit when the metro is chosen.
- **Dropped:** eLocal, Networx, Porch, Yelp, Bark. Same model as the kept per-lead platforms with less share of mind. Their prices sit inside the Angi and Thumbtack ranges and are used in Phase 9 as a cross-check.

### Selection rule applied

A competitor earns a slot only if the customer actually reaches for it in the moment.

- **Kept:** Google search and call down the list, as one column, because it is what the source itself names as the customer's default ("no googling, no calling down a list") and where every other option is found.
- **Kept:** Roto-Rooter, the national 24/7 plumbing brand most likely to appear in that search, with no after-hours surcharge (S41).
- **Kept:** Benjamin Franklin Plumbing, because its punctuality guarantee is the closest existing analog to the 90-minute guarantee (S40), which makes it the comparison Phase 2 has to answer.
- **Kept:** Angi and Thumbtack, as the two quote marketplaces a customer finds next, with purchase guarantees SOS SMS does not have (S44, S45).
- **Kept as a sixth column:** American Home Shield, because for a covered failure a plan holder calls the warranty line first, and its own contract says it does not guarantee timely emergency service (S20).
- **Profiled but not a column:** InstaService (closest model, small footprint and review base, S53), Mr. Rooter and SERVPRO (same role as Roto-Rooter in search), Nextdoor and neighbors (slot 3, used in Phase 3 and Phase 8), DIY or wait (slot 5, the status quo in Phase 3), Frontdoor video chat (an adjacent "help me now" option).
