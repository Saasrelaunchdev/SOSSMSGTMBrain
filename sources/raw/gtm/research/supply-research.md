### What to watch for, and what was found

The brief's hypothesis was that contractors resent paying for leads that do not become jobs and resent showing up without getting paid. The first half is strongly supported: lead cost and lead quality dominate the negative themes for C1 to C4. The second half, unpaid trips, is plausible but not directly evidenced in the sources, so it stays a hypothesis for interviews.

The warning the research pack predicted also came true. The second most common complaint, after cost, is that **the same request goes to several contractors**. SOS SMS sends each request to every matched contractor at once. That is a weakness Phase 3 has to answer, not a strength it can claim. The answer the run uses: on SOS SMS, a contractor who does not get the job pays nothing for having seen it, which is the specific harm in the shared-lead complaint.
