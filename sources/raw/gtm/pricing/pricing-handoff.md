## Strategic rationale

**Who pays.** SOS SMS is a two-sided network, and supply is the side that is hard to get: every member promise depends on enough contractors in the zone to reach the density number. So contractors are subsidized, and members, who pay to be ready before the need, fund the network. Contractors start paying only after the founding period, and only when a job is completed and paid.

**Contractors: free to join, per completed job later.** $0 to join, $0 per request seen or declined, no subscription, no seat fee. After the founding period a contractor fee of $25 per completed paid job is deducted from the trip charge payout. Founding contractors keep $0 a job for 12 months after the founding period, written into the founding agreement from day one. The fee is built from the floor up and held far under the market: the cheapest per-lead option costs about $125 per booked job at a 20 percent close rate (S03, S06, grade C, internal only), so the fee leaves the contractor about $100 better off per booked job. It sits at the value ceiling (20 percent of that lead spend) and clears the cost floor ($10), leaving 86% of the fee as margin.

**Members: a membership, then one text.** Anyone who texts gets free help: the safety line, the stop-the-water steps, and how to join. Members pay a membership, monthly or annual, with the card and home address on file from joining, so in an emergency one text starts dispatch. The $99 trip charge goes on the card on file when a contractor accepts, is paid to the contractor, and is credited to the repair if the member goes ahead. There is no fee per request. The planning price, $10 a month, sits at the closest analog's price (a plumber's club, S75), inside the band of emergency memberships and plumbing plans ($4.83 to $22.99 a month, S75 to S78, S80), and far above its cost floor ($3.75 a month at a 65% margin), leaving about $8.51 a member a month of contribution in the founding period.

**Why the Hero columns are built this way.** For contractors the column to highlight is the founding offer: during recruiting every contractor who signs up is a founding contractor, and the offer is what makes signing up before launch worth it. For members the Hero is the membership; Help by text exists so value comes before payment, and so a non-member mid-emergency is never left without the safety line and the steps.

**What the numbers say about growth.** The first 6 months of a monthly member's contribution, about $43 at 6.5% monthly churn (R59), is the acquisition ceiling per new paid member (R63). An affiliate commission of $20 (R61) sits under it; paid search at the plumbing benchmark costs about $137 per new paid member (S15, grade B, with conversion assumed equal), about 3.2 times the ceiling. Affiliates and email lead; paid sign-ups support only where they come in under the ceiling. Retention, annual plans, and the contractor fee after the founding period are the levers.

## Notes for design

- **Two tables, two pages, never one.** The contractor table goes on the contractor page and the terms on one page; the member table goes on the membership page and the guarantee page. No page shows both.
- **Highlight** the Founding contractor column and the Membership column. Do not label either "Most popular": nothing is live.
- **A monthly or annual toggle on the membership page only.** Monthly is the default; annual shows "two months free". The contractor side has no subscription, so no toggle and no "contact sales".
- **The membership price and the trip charge are always separate lines.** The membership is paid before the need; the trip charge only on use. Never show one total.
- **Prices are merge fields.** `{{monthly price}}` and `{{annual price}}` come from the zone's price record, set in Stage 0; the planning values stay internal until then.
- **Cancellation and renewal terms sit next to the price**, in plain words, before the card is taken (S87).
- **The safety line** stays on the member table, and on any page that invites an urgent text.
- **Contractor copy is future tense before launch** ("when SOS SMS opens in {{zone}}"). Member pages stay held until memberships open in the zone.
- **Merge fields.** {{SOS SMS number}} and {{zone}} are filled from the registered number and the zone record when a page is built; the covered areas and hours come from the same zone record.
- **Grade C figures stay internal.** Competitor prices, churn benchmarks, and affiliate market rates live in the model, not on any page.
