## Strategic rationale

**Who pays.** Supply is the side that is hard to get, so contractors enter free and pay only from revenue a job already produced; members, who pay to be ready before the need, fund the network.

**The denominator and the basis.** Members pay per household, a month or a year, on one of three plans, plus the trip charge on use. The basis is the market for now: the member band runs $4.83 to $36.95 a month (median $16.99), and the hard saving on a visit (the after-hours call fee avoided, about $101.00, S50, grade C) supports only a small part of the price, so members pay for readiness. Cost is the floor on every plan, at an 80 percent margin target. It moves to value-based once the launch zone measures what members save.

**Why the Hero is Plus, and why 80 percent.** Plus is the plan with the founder's guarantee: on a late arrival or a no-show, the membership paid in the 6 months before the request comes back as a credit on the next bills (up to $90 on the monthly plan, $75 on the annual plan). It adds the household benefit: up to 5 phones can text for the home, so whoever is there sends the text. Plus costs $5 a month more than Basic, a small step for the two things a household notices. Premier, at 2.33 times Plus, anchors the ladder: it is the plan for two homes or heavy use, and it makes Plus the reasonable choice. Plus sits just under the market median, low in its workable range on purpose.

**Basic, the Decoy.** One text, three trades, one phone, no credit. The method's Decoy at half the Hero ($7.50) sits under Basic's own cost floor ($7.69), so Basic sits at the closest analog's price, a plumber's club at $10 a month (S75).

**Premier, the Anchor.** Everything in Plus, with a 12-month credit (up to $420 monthly, $350 annual), one trip charge a membership year paid by SOS SMS (on the monthly plan after 6 paid months), a second home, and up to 10 phones.

**The two ways the 90 minutes are missed.** No contractor accepts: the member pays nothing and is told within 15 minutes, no contractor is paid, and the trip money nets to zero. The contractor arrives late: the trip charge stands, because it pays for the work, and Plus and Premier members get their credit. A contractor who accepts and does not come is paid nothing, and the trip charge goes back to the member.

**What the numbers say.** At the plan mix a member contributes about $14.91 a month (Basic $9.29, Plus $13.77, Premier $29.62). The acquisition ceiling, the first 6 months of contribution, is about $47 Basic, $70 Plus, and $151 Premier. A $20 affiliate commission sits under every ceiling. Paid search at the plumbing benchmark costs about $137 per new paid member (S15, grade B), 1.8 times the blended ceiling, so there is no paid search at launch (decision 11). One zone of about 411 members contributes about $6,134 a month on monthly billing.
