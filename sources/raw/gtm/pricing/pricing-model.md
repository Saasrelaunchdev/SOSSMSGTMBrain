## The direction

Contractors join free: $0 to join, $0 per request seen or declined, no subscription, no seat fee. SOS SMS earns only on transactions: an SOS SMS fee on each customer request from the first one, and a contractor fee per completed paid job once the founding period ends. Founding contractors keep $0 a job for 12 months after the founding period. One model prices both sides.

## Strategy

<!-- sheet: Strategy -->
| Decision | Choice | One-line rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Goal balance | The lowest barrier for contractors; SOS SMS earns only when a transaction happens | Supply is the constrained side, and every customer promise depends on density. Contractors pay nothing to join, to see a request, or to decline one. SOS SMS is paid when a job happens: by the customer on each request, and by the contractor per completed job once the founding period ends. |
| Stage (When) | Pre-launch, Stage 0 to Stage 1 | Nothing is live, so pricing is a learning instrument. Keep it simple, measure acceptance and the text-to-paid rate in the founding zone, and check the contractor fee against the founding contractors' real close rates before it starts. |
| Beachhead (Who), contractors | Licensed plumbing and water-emergency shops in one zone of one metro (decision 1) | Easy to serve by text, and SOS SMS has no share yet. The founding cohort is sized by the density number for each hour band, not by a quota. |
| Beachhead (Who), customers | Homeowners in the founding zone with an urgent plumbing or water problem, in covered hours | Reached at the moment they search, and only where the density number is met. The demand side is homeowners; property managers would be a separate run. |
| Sales motion | Product-led on both sides (gathering): free entry, self-serve by text, no sales team | Decided 2026-10-01. Contractors join by texting JOIN; customers get stop-the-water help by text before any card. No plan needs a call, so no plan is "contact sales". |
| Strategic intent | Differentiate on mechanics (market shaker) | Lead platforms and warranty networks set the category's pricing norms. SOS SMS does not contest volume or exclusivity; it changes who pays and when: nothing to see a request, and the trip paid before the contractor rolls (positioning verdict, decided 2026-10-01). |
| Which side pays | Contractors are subsidized. Customers pay per request. Contractors pay per completed job after the founding period | The side that is hardest to get is subsidized, and the side that captures urgent value pays. The customer pays for speed and the 90-minute guarantee, and the refund liability sits on that side too (both runs' Phase 9). |
| Denominator, contractors | Per completed paid job. $0 to join, $0 per request, $0 per technician | The contractor pays only when revenue exists. Per request is the per-lead model contractors are leaving; per seat or per month breaks "no subscription". |
| Denominator, customers | Per request, as two lines before anyone is sent: the trip charge and the SOS SMS fee | One request is one emergency handled. A flat price stated before dispatch avoids the after-hours surprise fee customers resent (S50, S51). The two lines are never one number. |
| Pricing basis, contractors | Cost floor up, held under the market: a small share of the lead spend a job no longer needs | The subsidized side is priced from the floor up, never into the market band. The cheapest per-lead option, about $125 per booked job at a 20 percent close rate (S03, S06, grade C), is the ceiling to stay under. |
| Pricing basis, customers | Value-based, with the market as the reference band and cost as the floor | The fee is set against what a customer pays to get someone out at night (S50) and must cover the guarantee's refunds, card processing, messaging, and support. |
| Denominator x basis fit, contractors | Pass | Per completed job is an outcome denominator; it pairs with value-based pricing and tracks value most tightly. Metering is simple because SOS SMS already holds the trip charge. |
| Denominator x basis fit, customers | Pass | Per request is a usage denominator; it pairs with market-based and value-based pricing. The guarantee makes the request price outcome-backed. |
| Founding period and the switch to transaction revenue (note) | Founding contractors pay $0 a job through the founding period and 12 months after. Contractors who join after the founding list closes pay $0 a job until the founding period ends, then the contractor fee | Written into the founding agreement from day one, so nobody is surprised (both runs' Phase 9). The fee is deducted from the trip charge payout on completed paid jobs only; a declined or lost request never costs anything. |
| Rejected models (note) | Per request to contractors; subscription or per seat; an after-hours premium; paid priority | Each breaks a stated promise: no per-lead fee, no subscription, no surprise fee, and every matched contractor sees the request at once. A household membership is not offered at launch. |
| Status (note) | Decided 2026-10-03: decisions 2 and 5 | Every amount here is decided and may be used in copy (gtm/marketplace/resolution-register.md). Planning values are replaced by the founding zone's measured figures. Grade C market figures stay internal. Nothing is live. |
