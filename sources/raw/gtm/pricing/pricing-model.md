## The direction

Contractors join free and pay a $25 contractor fee per completed paid job, from the first job, in three trades (plumbing, HVAC, electrical). Members choose Basic ($10 a month), Plus ($15, most popular), or Premier ($35), monthly or annual with two months free, joined before the need, plus the $99 trip charge on use, credited to the repair. The plans carry sliding-scale entitlements: the late-arrival credit (none on Basic, 6 months on Plus, 12 on Premier), the phones that can text for the home (1, up to 5, up to 10), the homes covered, and the trip charges SOS SMS pays. Plus is built for 80 percent of members.

| Sales motion | Product-led (gathering): self-serve joining, no sales team | Members join on the membership page or from Text READY; contractors text JOIN. | Strategic intent | Differentiate (market shaker) | Plumber's clubs, utility-partner plans, and lead platforms set the category's norms. SOS SMS follows the membership norm (a three-plan ladder, two months free on the annual plan) and differentiates on mechanics: one text reaches every matched contractor, the trip is paid before anyone rolls, and the arrival time is backed on Plus and Premier. |
| Denominator, members | Per household, a month or a year, on one of three plans; the trip charge on use | A household is the unit that has the emergency. Per household works like per device: it fits market-based and value-based pricing. The plans differ by what stands behind the arrival time and by who in the home can text, never by how fast a request goes out. |
| Denominator, contractors | Per completed paid job | The contractor pays only when revenue exists: an outcome denominator. Per request is the per-lead model contractors are leaving. |
