## The direction

Contractors join free: $0 to join, $0 per request seen or declined, no subscription, no seat fee, and a contractor fee per completed paid job once the founding period ends. Members pay a monthly or annual membership, joined before the need, planning values $10 a month or $100 a year, plus the $99 trip charge on use, credited to the repair; there is no fee per request. Founding contractors keep $0 a job for 12 months after the founding period. One model prices both sides.

## Strategy

<!-- sheet: Strategy -->
| Decision | Choice | One-line rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Goal balance | The lowest barrier for contractors; members fund the network before the need | Supply is the constrained side, and every member promise depends on density. Contractors pay nothing to join, to see a request, or to decline one. Members pay a monthly or annual membership, joined before anything breaks, so the emergency is one text; contractors pay per completed job once the founding period ends. |
| Stage (When) | Pre-launch, Stage 0 to Stage 1 | Nothing is live, so pricing is a learning instrument. The membership price is a planning value until the launch price is set in Stage 0. Measure acceptance, churn, and cost per new paid member in the founding zone, and check the contractor fee against the founding contractors' real close rates before it starts. |
| Beachhead (Who), contractors | Licensed plumbing and water-emergency shops in one zone of one metro (decision 1) | Easy to serve by text, and SOS SMS has no share yet. The founding cohort is sized by the density number for each hour band, not by a quota. |
| Beachhead (Who), members | Homeowners in the founding zone who want to be ready before something breaks, once memberships open at the density number | Reached before the need, through affiliates and email, and only where the density number is met (R55). The demand side is homeowners; property managers would be a separate run. |
| Sales motion | Product-led on both sides (gathering): free entry for contractors, self-serve joining for members, no sales team | Contractors join by texting JOIN; members join on the membership page or from Text READY, reached through affiliates, email, and search for sign-ups. No plan needs a call, so no plan is "contact sales". |
| Strategic intent | Differentiate on mechanics (market shaker) | Lead platforms, warranties, and plumber clubs set the category's pricing norms. SOS SMS does not contest volume, exclusivity, or repair coverage; it changes who pays and when: members pay to be ready before the need, contractors pay nothing to see a request, and the trip is paid before the contractor rolls. |
| Which side pays | Contractors are subsidized. Members pay a membership. Contractors pay per completed job after the founding period | The side that is hard to get is subsidized, and the side that values readiness pays. Members pay for one-text dispatch and the 90-minute guarantee before the need, and the refund liability sits inside the membership. |
| Denominator, contractors | Per completed paid job. $0 to join, $0 per request, $0 per technician | The contractor pays only when revenue exists. Per request is the per-lead model contractors are leaving; per seat or per month breaks "no subscription". |
| Denominator, members | Per household, monthly or annual; the $99 trip charge on use | A member pays to be ready every month, whether or not anything breaks. The trip charge is paid on use and credited to the repair; there is no fee per request (decision 2, revised 2026-10-11). |
| Pricing basis, contractors | Cost floor up, held under the market: a small share of the lead spend a job no longer needs | The subsidized side is priced from the floor up, never into the market band. The cheapest per-lead option, about $125 per booked job at a 20 percent close rate (S03, S06, grade C), is the ceiling to stay under. |
| Pricing basis, members | Analog-based, with the market band as the reference and cost as the floor | The planning price sits at the closest analog's price, a plumber's club at $10 a month (S75), inside the band of emergency memberships and plumbing plans, and must cover card processing, messaging, support, and each member's share of request costs, including the guarantee's refunds. |
| Denominator x basis fit, contractors | Pass | Per completed job is an outcome denominator; it pairs with value-based pricing and tracks value most tightly. Metering is simple because SOS SMS already holds the trip charge. |
| Denominator x basis fit, members | Pass | Per household is a subscription denominator; it pairs with analog and value-based pricing. The guarantee makes the membership outcome-backed. |
| Founding period and the switch to transaction revenue (note) | Founding contractors pay $0 a job through the founding period and 12 months after. Contractors who join after the founding list closes pay $0 a job until the founding period ends, then the contractor fee | Written into the founding agreement from day one, so nobody is surprised (both runs' Phase 9). The fee is deducted from the trip charge payout on completed paid jobs only; a declined or lost request never costs anything. |
| Rejected models (note) | Per request to contractors; subscription or per seat for contractors; a fee per request for members; an after-hours premium; paid priority | Each breaks a stated promise: no per-lead fee, no subscription for contractors, one text with nothing to agree to at the worst moment, no surprise fee, and every matched contractor sees the request at once. The pay per request model was retired on 2026-10-11. |
