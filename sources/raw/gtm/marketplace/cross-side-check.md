# Cross-side check: SOS SMS supply and demand runs

Both briefs end with the same rule: neither side's copy may promise something the other side's terms do not deliver. This file runs that check across both completed runs, row by row against `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 6, then lists the conflicts the runs uncovered that Section 6 did not anticipate.

Inputs: `../run-1-supply-contractors/output/` and `../run-2-demand-customers/output/`, both 2026-10-01. Every decision in Part 3 was decided on 2026-10-03; the values and their basis are in `resolution-register.md`.

**Revised 2026-10-11, the membership revision.** The demand side is a monthly or annual membership, joined before the need, and a member's one text starts dispatch. Rows 1, 2, and 8, conflicts B, D, F, G, and H, and decisions 2, 3, 6, 8, 11, and 12 carry the revised words. Every row is still consistent or closed.

**Revised 2026-10-20, the tiers revision.** One membership covers plumbing, HVAC, and electrical; members choose Basic, Plus, or Premier; a late arrival keeps its trip charge, and the 90-minute guarantee on Plus and Premier is a credit of 6 months of membership on Plus and 12 on Premier (12 since 2026-10-21); no paid search at launch. Rows 1, 2, and 8, conflicts B, F, and G, and decisions 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, and 12 carry the revised words. Every row is still consistent or closed.

## Part 2. Conflicts the runs uncovered

Not in Section 6. Each is a place where one side's plan, as written, would break a promise on the other side.

| # | Conflict | Where it appears | Consequence if ignored | Resolution, decided |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Who sets the trip charge.** Run 2 recommends one SOS SMS trip charge per trade and zone, so the member sees one price before joining. Run 1's positioning tells contractors "your price for the work stays yours" | Run 1 Phase 3; Run 2 Phase 9 decision 3 | A contractor who expects to set their own trip charge feels misled | Adopt one trip charge per trade and zone, and say so in the terms on one page. Contractor copy keeps "your price for the work" and never says "your trip charge" |
| B | **Accepting with an arrival time outside the window.** The 90 minutes run from the member's request text; a contractor accepting late in the window can only commit to a time past it | Run 1 Phase 9 option C; Run 2 drill 3 | Late arrivals become unfair to contractors, or the guarantee breaks at the moment of acceptance | Dispatch only lets a contractor accept with an arrival time inside the remaining window. When no contractor can, nobody accepts: the member is told within 15 minutes and never charged (decision 3, revised 2026-10-20) |
| C | **Address before acceptance.** Contractors need to judge distance before they accept; members are promised that only the accepting contractor gets their address | Run 1 drills 7 to 9; Run 2 drill 9 | Either the privacy promise or the contractor's ability to commit to a time fails | Show an approximate distance or cross-street area before acceptance, the full address after |
| D | **Requests without a card.** Before the membership revision, Run 2 suggested a pay-on-arrival path for people who could not pay by text | Run 2 Phase 9; Run 1 VP2 | "Paid to show up" is false for those jobs | No pay-on-arrival path. Joining puts a card on file, with an optional backup card, so every member request has one; no dispatch without an authorized trip charge, and if both cards decline the member is told at once and dispatch starts the moment a card authorizes; a text from a non-member gets the free help by text and pays nothing (R16, R57) |
| E | **A contractor's own customers.** Run 2 suggests contractors point their own customers to SOS SMS membership for after-hours cover; Run 1 promises the customer relationship stays with the contractor | Run 2 Phase 7 acceleration; Run 1 item 7 | A contractor's own customer, once a member, could be accepted by a rival | No first refusal (R13, retired 2026-10-11): every request goes to all matched contractors at once, because speed is the product. A contractor may refer their own customers through the affiliate program on the same terms as any partner (R61), knowing the request goes to every matched contractor; the repair relationship stays theirs whenever they take the job |
| F | **Gas and other life-safety texts.** The member side holds them for the safety reply first | Run 2 rules; Run 1 dispatch | A plumber, HVAC technician, or electrician may be dispatched into a gas leak or a live fault | Requests mentioning gas, smoke, fire, sparks, a burning smell, a shock, or injury get the safety reply and are not broadcast until the member confirms it is safe and the utility or 911 has been called |
| G | **Memberships ahead of density.** Affiliates, email, and paid social sell memberships before the need (membership revision, 2026-10-11) | Run 2 Phase 8 | Members join in a zone nobody can reach in time in one of the three trades: late arrivals, then cancellations | Memberships open only in zones where each of the three trades meets Run 1's density number in every hour band (R15, R55); before then, the launch list. Sign-ups pause automatically when acceptance in any trade falls: paid social stops and affiliate links show the launch list (R34) |
| H | **Payment hold timing.** Run 1 promises the trip charge is collected before the contractor rolls; card authorizations usually last 7 days online (S30) | Run 1 drill 4; Run 2 Phase 7 stage 5 | Mismatch between hold, capture, and payout produces disputes | Stripe Connect: the card saved at joining; authorize on the card on file when the member's request text arrives (the backup card if the first declines), capture at acceptance, hold for release on arrival, payout within 2 business days less the $25 contractor fee; refunds per the late-arrival rule |
