# Cross-side check: SOS SMS supply and demand runs

Both briefs end with the same rule: neither side's copy may promise something the other side's terms do not deliver. This file runs that check across both completed runs, row by row against `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 6, then lists the conflicts the runs uncovered that Section 6 did not anticipate.

Inputs: `../run-1-supply-contractors/output/` and `../run-2-demand-customers/output/`, both 2026-10-01. Every decision in Part 3 was decided on 2026-10-03; the values and their basis are in `resolution-register.md`.

**Revised 2026-10-11, the membership revision.** The demand side is a monthly or annual membership, joined before the need, and a member's one text starts dispatch. Rows 1, 2, and 8, conflicts B, D, F, G, and H, and decisions 2, 3, 6, 8, 11, and 12 carry the revised words. Every row is still consistent or closed.

## Part 2. Conflicts the runs uncovered

Not in Section 6. Each is a place where one side's plan, as written, would break a promise on the other side.

| # | Conflict | Where it appears | Consequence if ignored | Resolution, decided |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Who sets the trip charge.** Run 2 recommends one SOS SMS trip charge per trade and zone, so the member sees one price before joining. Run 1's positioning tells contractors "your price for the work stays yours" | Run 1 Phase 3; Run 2 Phase 9 decision 3 | A contractor who expects to set their own trip charge feels misled | Adopt one trip charge per trade and zone, and say so in the terms on one page. Contractor copy keeps "your price for the work" and never says "your trip charge" |
| B | **Accepting with an arrival time outside the window.** The guarantee runs from the member's request text; a contractor accepting late in the window can only commit to a time past it | Run 1 Phase 9 option C; Run 2 drill 3 | Fault-based refunds become unfair to contractors, or the guarantee breaks at the moment of acceptance | Dispatch only lets a contractor accept with an arrival time inside the remaining window. When no contractor can, the miss is SOS-caused and SOS SMS pays |
| C | **Address before acceptance.** Contractors need to judge distance before they accept; members are promised that only the accepting contractor gets their address | Run 1 drills 7 to 9; Run 2 drill 9 | Either the privacy promise or the contractor's ability to commit to a time fails | Show an approximate distance or cross-street area before acceptance, the full address after |
| D | **Requests without a card.** Before the membership revision, Run 2 suggested a pay-on-arrival path for people who could not pay by text | Run 2 Phase 9; Run 1 VP2 | "Paid to show up" is false for those jobs | No pay-on-arrival path. Joining puts a card on file, so every member request has one; a text from a non-member gets the free help by text and pays nothing (R16, R57) |
| E | **A contractor's own customers.** Run 2 suggests founding contractors point their own customers to SOS SMS membership for after-hours cover; Run 1 promises the customer relationship stays with the contractor | Run 2 Phase 7 acceleration; Run 1 item 7 | A contractor's own customer, once a member, could be accepted by a rival, which ends that contractor's participation | Do not use this acceleration until dispatch can give the referring contractor first refusal on their own customers' requests for 5 minutes. Until then, drop it |
| F | **Gas and other life-safety texts.** The member side holds them for the safety reply first | Run 2 rules; Run 1 dispatch | A plumber may be dispatched into a gas leak | Requests mentioning gas, smoke, fire, or injury get the safety reply and are not broadcast until the member confirms it is safe and the utility or 911 has been called |
| G | **Memberships ahead of density.** Affiliates, email, and search sell memberships before the need (membership revision, 2026-10-11) | Run 2 Phase 8 | Members join in a zone nobody can reach in time: refunds, then cancellations | Memberships open only in zones at Run 1's density number, for their covered hours (R55); before then, the launch list. Sign-ups pause automatically when acceptance falls: ads stop and affiliate links show the launch list (R34) |
| H | **Payment hold timing.** Run 1 promises the trip charge is collected before the contractor rolls; card authorizations usually last 7 days online (S30) | Run 1 drill 4; Run 2 Phase 7 stage 5 | Mismatch between hold, capture, and payout produces disputes | Stripe Connect: the card saved at joining; authorize on the card on file when the member's request text arrives, capture at acceptance, hold for release on arrival, payout within 2 business days; refunds per the late-arrival rule |

## Result

All eight Section 6 rows are consistent or closed: rows 1 and 7 closed with decisions 3 and 7, not rewrites, and row 2 stays out of copy until the relaunch is built. The eight conflicts in Part 2 are resolved, each keeping both sides' copy as written in Phase 6. With the twelve decisions made, and six of them revised by the membership revision on 2026-10-11, every line that waited on one carries the decided words; contractor recruiting copy stays in the future tense, and member copy stays held until memberships open in its zone.
