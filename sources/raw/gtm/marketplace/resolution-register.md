# Resolution register

Every unknown and every open founder decision in the SOS SMS go-to-market, resolved in one place. Written 2026-10-03 by the GTM consultant, at the instruction to resolve everything that public data, the research, and the GTM itself can resolve, and to leave no unknown and no decision open in any deliverable. Every file in this repository, the GTM brain, the walkthrough deck, and the Google folder now uses these values.

**What this changes.** The twelve decisions in `cross-side-check.md` Part 3 are decided, and copy that waited on them carries the decided words. Every unknown number has a value with its basis, or the method and the moment that produce it.

**Revised 2026-10-11, the membership revision: one model, built for profitability.** The demand side is a monthly or annual membership, joined before the need, with the card and home address on file, so in an emergency a member sends one text and dispatch starts. Every contractor and every member is on the same terms from the first day: the founding period, the founding terms, and the founding list are retired, and the contractor fee applies from the first completed paid job. Decisions 2, 3, 6, 8, and 11 carry revised words, and decision 12 is replaced by an acquisition ceiling per new paid member. Retired, with their rows kept so earlier references still resolve: R02 (the SOS SMS fee), R05 (founding terms), R06 (founding period), R07 (founding list), R13 (first refusal), and R30 (first text to paid). R01, R03, R08, R09, R11, R15 to R19, R21, R23 to R25, R31, R32, R34, R39, R42, R43, R46, R49, and R50 are revised; R51 to R64 are new. The merge fields add `{{renewal date}}`; `{{founding close date}}` and `{{covered hours}}` are retired. Nothing is left open: every question the membership raised is decided here.

**Revised 2026-10-20, the tiers revision: three trades, three plans, no paid search.** One membership covers plumbing, HVAC, and electrical emergencies, and a zone opens only when all three trades meet the density number in every hour band. Members choose Basic ($10 a month or $100 a year), Plus ($15 or $150), or Premier ($25 or $250); the plans differ by what stands behind the arrival time, never by how fast a request goes out. A late arrival keeps its trip charge, because it pays for the work; on Plus and Premier, the 90-minute guarantee credits the membership paid in the 6 months before the request. No paid search at launch. Decisions 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, and 12 carry revised words. R01, R03, R08 to R10, R15, R17, R19, R21, R25, R29, R32, R34, R40, R42, R51 to R55, R57, R58, R61, R63, and R64 are revised; R65 to R68 are new. The merge fields add `{{plan}}` and `{{credit amount}}`.

**Revised 2026-10-21, the pricing revision: three plans with sliding-scale entitlements, built with the bottom-up pricing method.** The plans keep their order and their roles: Basic the entry plan, Plus the plan built for 80 percent of members, Premier the anchor. The late-arrival credit slides by plan: none on Basic, the membership paid in the 6 months before the request on Plus (the founder's rule), and in the 12 months before it on Premier. A new household benefit slides with it: the phones that can text for the home, the member's phone on Basic, up to 5 on Plus, and up to 10 across two homes on Premier. Premier moves to $35 a month or $350 a year, 2.33 times Plus, so it anchors the ladder; Basic and Plus keep their prices. The pricing model is rebuilt around one sheet of founder inputs that drives every other sheet (`../pricing/pricing-model.md`). Decisions 2, 3, and 12 carry revised words. R09, R19, R31, R53, R54, R63 to R66 are revised; R69 and R70 are new.

**What this does not change.** SOS SMS is pre-launch: nothing is live, and nothing here is a result. No member, contractor, job, arrival time, testimonial, or proof point is invented. Grade C figures stay internal. Contractor recruiting copy stays in the future tense, and member copy stays held until memberships open in its zone.

## Five kinds of resolution

| Kind | What it means | How copy treats it |
|---|---|---|
| **Decided** | A choice SOS SMS makes: a price, a rule, a term. Set here, with its basis | Used as written, in copy on the side it belongs to |
| **Planning value** | A number SOS SMS plans with: a target, a threshold, or an operating assumption, with its basis | Used in planning and internal math. Never presented as a measured result. The measured figure replaces it once a zone runs |
| **Counted at** | A fact that cannot exist before launch, resolved to the method that produces it and the stage when it is produced | The method is stated; no number is written until it is counted |
| **Merge field** | A value that differs per zone, per contractor, per member, or per message, written `{{field}}` and filled from a named record when the message is sent | Never typed by hand; the field list is below |
| **Retired** | A value a later decision removed. The row stays, with the date and what replaced it, so earlier references still resolve | Never used in copy |

## Arithmetic, checked

From the pricing model (`../pricing/pricing-model.md`); every line below is a formula on its sheets.

- A member pays a plan, Basic $10 a month or $100 a year, Plus $15 or $150, Premier $35 or $350, and the $99 trip charge only on use, credited to the repair. Never shown as one total in copy.
- Requests per member a year: plumbing 0.25 + HVAC 0.20 + electrical 0.05 = 0.50; Premier, with a second home, 1.5 × 0.50 = 0.75. Accepted: 90 percent (R32); completed paid jobs: 95 percent of those (R29), so 0.43 a year on Basic and Plus, 0.64 on Premier.
- Per completed job: card processing on the trip charge 2.9% × $99 + $0.30 = $3.17; messages 30 × $0.01 = $0.30; member support and platform $5.00; contractor payout, checks, and support $3.50. Total $11.97. SOS SMS's revenue is the $25 contractor fee, so each job nets about $13.03. The contractor receives $74 of the $99.
- No contractor accepts: the member is never charged and no contractor is paid, so the trip money nets to $0; SOS SMS's cost is the request's handling, $5.30.
- Basic contribution a month: $10 - $0.59 processing - $0.05 messaging - $0.50 support - $0.46 requests + $0.89 contractor fee = $9.29.
- Plus contribution a month: $15 - $0.74 - $0.25 messaging for 5 phones - $0.50 - $0.46 + $0.89 - the credit 0.45 accepted × 0.05 × 6 months × $15 ÷ 12 ($0.17) = $13.77.
- Premier contribution a month: $35 - $1.32 - $0.50 messaging for 10 phones - $0.50 - $0.70 + $1.34 - the credit 0.675 × 0.05 × 12 × $35 ÷ 12 ($1.18) - the covered trip charge 0.935^6 × (1 - e^-0.641) × ($99 - $3.17) ÷ 12 ($2.53) = $29.62.
- Cost floors at an 80 percent margin: Basic about $7.69, Plus $9.48, Premier $32.95 a month; every price clears its floor.
- Annual contribution: Basic $95.31, Plus $139.78, Premier $278.05 a year; at 75 percent renewal about $381, $559, and $1,112 over the membership.
- Acquisition ceilings: contribution × (1 - 0.935^6) ÷ 0.065 = contribution × 5.105: about $47, $70, and $151; on the annual plans, half a year: about $48, $70, and $139. Lifetime contribution, monthly plan: about $143, $212, and $456.
- The plan mix (R70), 10, 80, and 10 percent: a blended price of $16.50 and contribution of $14.91 a member a month; a blended ceiling of about $76.
- Members per zone: 9 × 12 ÷ (0.25 × 1.05) = 411, about 410. At that base: HVAC 0.8 requests a month per contractor; electrical 0.2. The zone contributes about $6,134 a month on monthly billing.
- Join Basic monthly, use once, cancel: the earliest dispatch comes after two payments, 2 × ($10 - $0.59 - $0.05 - $0.50) - $11.97 + $25 = $30.75, still positive.
- Plus monthly, 6 payments in, one late arrival, the credit used in full: 6 × ($15 - $0.74 - $0.25 - $0.50) + $13.03 - $90 = $4.12, still positive.
- Premier monthly, 12 payments in, one late arrival, the credit used in full: 12 × ($35 - $1.32 - $0.50 - $0.50) + $13.03 - $420 = about -$14.75: the one rare case that costs money, at 5 in 100 accepted requests; the expected cost is inside Premier's contribution.
- Premier monthly, 6 payments, the covered trip charge, then cancel: 6 × ($35 - $1.32 - $0.50 - $0.50) - $74 - $8.80 = $113.31. Premier annual, the covered trip charge in the first week: $350 - $10.45 - $6.00 - $6.00 - $82.80 = $244.75.
- Paid search: $10.49 a click ÷ 7.63 percent = about $137 per new paid member, 1.8 times the blended ceiling and 2.9 times Basic's (S15, B). No paid search at launch.
- Density at a = 0.30: n ≥ ln(1 - 0.90) ÷ ln(0.70) = 6.5, so 7; n ≥ ln(1 - 0.95) ÷ ln(0.70) = 8.4, so 9, in each trade: 27 in the first cohort.
- Zone radius: 90 - 15 - 20 = 55 minutes to drive; a 20-mile crossing at 25 miles an hour takes 48 minutes.
