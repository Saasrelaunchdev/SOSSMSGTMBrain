# Launch sequence: how the two GTMs fit together

The order the two GTMs execute in, and the gates between steps. Revised 2026-10-01 for the product-led motion: no sales team, contractors sign themselves up after cold email or a partner. Revised 2026-10-11, the membership revision: homeowners join as members before anything breaks, reached through affiliates and email first, with search and social for sign-ups, and a member's one text starts dispatch. Every element comes from the run outputs or from `speed-to-scale-plan.md`, cited by phase.

The organizing rule from both runs: **supply leads, memberships follow, zone by zone.** The member side's headline promises are only true where the contractor side has reached density (Run 1 Phase 5; Run 2 Phase 5), so memberships open only where they are true (R55). Before then, the zone's launch list grows: email, no charge, no service promised.

## Stage 3. Prove the zone, then accelerate

Use the gates in `speed-to-scale-plan.md`, section 8:

1. **Zone proven:** every zone signal at its threshold for 4 consecutive weeks. Then raise affiliate and sign-up spend in the zone while cost per new paid member holds under the ceiling and acceptance holds, and extend covered hours.
2. **Zone full:** covered hours at the guarantee standard and members growing toward about 430 (R64). Then open the adjacent zone from the template: contractor list and emails, partner kit, launch list, affiliates, membership page.
3. **Metro proven:** three zones pass without founders stepping in. Then a second metro.
4. **Trade added:** plumbing density holds and a referred trade reaches its own density. Then a second trade behind the same membership.

**Slow down** in any zone when SOS-caused refunds, acceptance, monthly churn, cost per new paid member, or contractor activity cross their thresholds. Sign-ups pause automatically when acceptance falls: ads stop and affiliate links show the launch list (R34).

| If the plan changes to | Then |
|---|---|
| Memberships first, recruiting contractors to meet them | The 90-minute guarantee cannot launch with memberships, and members would pay for a promise nobody can keep. Launch without the guarantee and rewrite Run 2 Phase 6 VP1 |
| A contractor fee from day one | Run 1 drills 1 to 3 change; Run 1 Phase 9 Anchor 3 sets the ceiling |
| Google does not confirm eligibility | Affiliates and email carry sign-ups alone, with Microsoft Ads (check its policies too) and organic pages; the acquisition mix changes |
| Property managers as a demand segment | A third run, with a B2B buying committee and contracted-vendor competitors |
