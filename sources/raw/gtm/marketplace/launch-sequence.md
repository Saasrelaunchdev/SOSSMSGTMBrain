# Launch sequence: how the two GTMs fit together

The order the two GTMs execute in, and the gates between steps. Revised 2026-10-01 for the product-led motion: no sales team, contractors sign themselves up after cold email or a partner, customers arrive through bottom-of-funnel search and the product loops. Every element comes from the run outputs or from `speed-to-scale-plan.md`, cited by phase.

The organizing rule from both runs: **supply leads, demand follows, zone by zone.** The customer side's headline promises are only true where the contractor side has reached density (Run 1 Phase 5; Run 2 Phase 5), and paid search only runs where they are true.

**Measures watched weekly, both sides together:**

| Measure | Side | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Active contractors per trade, zone, and hour band against the density target | Supply | Decides whether ads may run in that hour |
| Time from first text to acceptance; share with no acceptance in time | Both | The guarantee's real cost |
| Share arriving within the window; SOS-caused refund share | Both | Prices the fee (Run 2 Phase 9) |
| Blended cost per paid job; owned share of requests | Demand | Decides whether the zone's spend grows (speed-to-scale plan, section 5) |
| Days to first paid trip; share of trips paid before rolling | Supply | Keeps contractors active (Run 1 Phase 7) |

## Stage 3. Prove the zone, then accelerate

Use the gates in `speed-to-scale-plan.md`, section 8:

1. **Zone proven:** every zone signal at its threshold for the agreed number of weeks. Then raise ad budgets in the zone as far as acceptance holds, and extend covered hours.
2. **Zone full:** covered hours at the guarantee standard and owned share rising. Then open the adjacent zone from the template: contractor list and emails, partner kit, ad template, landing page.
3. **Metro proven:** three zones pass without founders stepping in. Then a second metro.
4. **Trade added:** plumbing density holds and a referred trade reaches its own density. Then a second trade behind the same number.

**Slow down** in any zone when SOS-caused refunds, acceptance, contractor activity, or cost per paid job cross their thresholds.

## What would change this sequence

| If the founders decide | Then |
|---|---|
| Demand first, recruiting contractors to meet it | The 90-minute guarantee cannot launch with demand, and paid search would buy requests nobody can serve. Launch without the guarantee and rewrite Run 2 Phase 6 VP1 |
| A contractor fee from day one | Run 1 drills 1 to 3 change; Run 1 Phase 9 Anchor 3 sets the ceiling |
| Google does not confirm eligibility | Lead with Microsoft Ads (check its policies too) and problem pages; the paid economics change |
| Property managers as a demand segment | A third run, with a B2B buying committee and contracted-vendor competitors |
