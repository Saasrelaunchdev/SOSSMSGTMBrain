# Phase 6. Value Proposition Dashboard, contractor side

Three pillars, nine reasons to believe, nine copy drills. Written to `../GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 5 (narrowed claims) and checked against Section 6 (cross-side dependencies).

**The fabrication rule for this phase.** SOS SMS has no contractors, jobs, or payouts. Every "Headline (Social Proof)" and every "Supporting Proof Point" is {TBD}. Where useful, a method substitute follows: a line that describes how the process works, which signals seriousness without claiming a result. No drill contains an earnings, volume, or "more jobs" claim.

**Brackets** mark text that cannot be finalized until an open decision is settled. Copy with an open bracket does not go out.

## Step 6.1. Brand promise and positioning

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| Brand Promise | Emergency jobs you choose, with the trip paid before you roll |
| Positioning | The emergency dispatch line where seeing a request costs nothing. Built around two things lead platforms do not do: no charge to receive a request, and the trip charge collected before the contractor drives out |

## Proof capture plan

Because the eighteen proof fields are empty, the first founding contractors are the first chance to fill them honestly:

1. The permission clause from Phase 5 is signed before each contractor's first job.
2. After each founding contractor's fifth job, an automated text asks three questions: was the trip paid before you rolled, did anything surprise you, would you tell another contractor. Answers are recorded verbatim with the date.
3. A row enters the proof table in `../GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 4 only when the proof point, its exact wording, its source, and the recorded permission all exist.
