## Something broke at home? Text us.
(Text button: "Something broke at home:") {{SOS SMS number}}

For fire, a gas smell, or anyone hurt, call 911 first.

### How it works, in three texts
1. You text what broke and where.
2. The licensed, insured plumbers on SOS SMS near you see it at once. The first who can come accepts and texts you an arrival time.
3. You agree to the trip charge before anyone is sent.

### What it costs
The trip charge is $99, and you see it before anyone is sent. If you go ahead with the repair, it counts toward the bill. There is also a $49 SOS SMS fee, charged only when a plumber accepts. The repair price comes from the plumber, on site.

### The 90-minute guarantee
If they are not there within 90 minutes of your request, the $99 trip charge comes back to you, automatically. Covered in {{zone neighborhoods}}, {{covered hours}}. If no plumber accepts, we tell you within 15 minutes and you pay nothing. (Link: read the guarantee in full.)

### Who we are
SOS SMS dispatches licensed, insured contractors. The contractor does the work. We check license and insurance before they can take a job, and again every six months.

### Your information
Only the plumber who takes your job gets your address and details. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for ads.

## Why this works
The text button and the safety line come first, because the reader may be standing in water. Each section answers one question in the order the customer asks it: how, how much, what if they're late, who are you, who sees my address. Every price and rule is decided and stated once; only the zone's own details (number, neighborhoods, hours) are merge fields from the zone record. The page never calls SOS SMS a plumber or an emergency service.
