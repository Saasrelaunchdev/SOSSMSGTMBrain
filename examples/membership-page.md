### How it works in one text
1. You join once, now, with your card and your home address.
2. When something breaks, you text what happened to {{SOS SMS number}}. That one text is the whole request.
3. The licensed, insured plumbers on SOS SMS near you see it at once. The first who can come accepts and texts you an arrival time, with their name and their Google rating where they have one.

### What it costs
Membership: {{monthly price}} a month, or {{annual price}} a year.

When you use it: a $99 trip charge, on your card when a plumber accepts. It counts toward the repair if you go ahead.

There is no fee per request. The repair price comes from the plumber, on site.

### The 90-minute guarantee
If your plumber is not there within 90 minutes of your text, the $99 trip charge comes back to you automatically, in {{zone neighborhoods}} during {{covered hours}}. If no plumber can come, we tell you within 15 minutes and nothing is charged. (Link: read the guarantee in full.)

### What membership includes, and what it doesn't
Included: one-text dispatch to the vetted plumbers on SOS SMS near you; an arrival time when one accepts; the 90-minute guarantee; a person by text when you text HELP, with a reply within 15 minutes in covered hours; the readiness kit, which is the shutoff guide, Text READY, and the contact card.

Not included: the repair, parts, or a guarantee on the work. SOS SMS is not a warranty or insurance. A warranty pays for covered repairs; SOS SMS gets a vetted plumber to your door.

### Cancel any time
Cancel on this page, or text HELP and a person will cancel it for you. A monthly membership ends at the end of the month you paid for. Annual members get a reminder before {{renewal date}}, with the price and how to cancel.

### Who we are
SOS SMS dispatches licensed, insured contractors. The contractor does the work. We check license and insurance before they can take a job, every six months, and on each policy's expiry date.

### Your information
Only the plumber who takes your job gets your address. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for ads.

## Why this works
- **Written for a calm reader.** People land here from a partner, an email, a guide, or a readiness search, before anything breaks. So the page sells being ready, not dispatch tonight: the promise, then the ZIP check, because memberships exist only in open zones (R55).
- **The safety line still sits at the top**, because some readers arrive in the middle of a problem.
- **Each section answers one question, in the order a careful buyer asks it:** how it works, what it costs, what if they are late, what it does not do, how to leave, who you are, who sees my address.
- **Two prices, never one total.** The membership, and the trip charge only when used, credited to the repair (decisions 2, 5, and 6).
- **The weak point answered before the card.** A membership costs something every month whether or not anything breaks (R46). The page answers with what it includes, the trip charge only on use, and cancel any time, all above the join button (S87).
- **It never calls SOS SMS a plumber, a warranty, insurance, or an emergency service**, and the prices are merge fields filled from the zone's price record (R51, R52).
