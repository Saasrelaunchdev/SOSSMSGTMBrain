When something breaks, you text what happened, and the licensed, insured contractors on SOS SMS for that trade near you see it at once; the first who can come accepts and texts you an arrival time.
>
> There are three plans: Basic, $100 a year or $10 a month; Plus, $150 or $15; Premier, $350 or $35. Cancel any time. On Plus and Premier, the 90-minute guarantee: if the contractor is late, SOS SMS credits the membership you paid for the 6 months (Premier: 12 months) before your request to your next bills. Covered at any hour, anywhere in {{zone neighborhoods}}.

> For fire, a gas smell, or anyone hurt, call 911 first.

## 6. What not to say
- Never "warranty", "insurance", "protection plan", or "covers repairs": the membership pays for no repair or parts.
- Never "24/7": an open zone is covered at any hour, said as "any hour, in {{zone neighborhoods}}". Never an arrival average, never "fast", and never one plan as faster than another.
- Never a price other than the three plans as written, and never the guarantee on Basic: it is named with Plus and Premier only.
- Never "SOS" alone: always the full name, SOS SMS.
- Never drop the disclosure line, and never move it to a footnote.

## Why this works
- **The partner reaches people before the need**, so every piece sells being ready, never help tonight.
- **The disclosure travels with every mention** (S88), in plain words a reader cannot miss.
- **The kit is the only words a partner needs**, so every claim stays the brain's: the trip charge on use, the guarantee with its scope, cancel any time, not a warranty.
- **A code on every link** makes new paid members countable by partner, which is how the commission is paid (R61) and how the channel is judged against the ceiling of the plan each member joins (R63).
- **The commission stays in the agreement.** A homeowner reads that the partner is paid, never the amount.
