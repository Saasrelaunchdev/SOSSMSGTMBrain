# Example: affiliate kit, for a homeowner association (members)

Status: **voice reference, written 2026-10-11 for the membership revision.** Written from the brain with every value decided or a merge field. The commission is a planning value (R61) that lives in the agreement, never in the words a homeowner reads. Goes to a partner only after the affiliate terms are written (Stage 0) and the agreement is signed. Template: `templates/affiliate-kit.md`.

Partner: {{partner}}, a homeowner association inside {{zone name}}. Every link is {{link}} carrying the partner's {{code}}, so each new paid member is counted to the partner.

Who gets this kit: partners SOS SMS may pay. A real estate agent, lender, title or settlement agent, or anyone referring at a home purchase settlement is never paid (S86, S94); they may share SOS SMS without a code or a fee.

## 6. What not to say
- Never "warranty", "insurance", "protection plan", or "covers repairs": the membership pays for no repair or parts.
- Never "24/7" unless the zone's covered hours are; never an arrival average, never "fast".
- Never a price typed by hand: the prices come from SOS SMS's price record, through the merge fields.
- Never "SOS" alone: always the full name, SOS SMS.
- Never drop the disclosure line, and never move it to a footnote.

---

## Why this works
- **The partner reaches people before the need**, so every piece sells being ready, never help tonight.
- **The disclosure travels with every mention** (S88), in plain words a reader cannot miss.
- **The kit is the only words a partner needs**, so every claim stays the brain's: the trip charge on use, the guarantee with its scope, cancel any time, not a warranty.
- **A code on every link** makes new paid members countable by partner, which is how the commission is paid (R61) and how the channel is judged against the ceiling of about $43 per new paid member (R63).
- **The commission stays in the agreement.** A homeowner reads that the partner is paid, never a planning number.
