# Example: the first reply (customers)

Status: **DRAFT, not approved.** Written from the brain for the voice reference. It ships only after decisions 3, 5, and 8 are logged, carrier registration is done, the shutoff steps are written and reviewed by a licensed plumber, and the founders approve it. Template: `templates/first-reply-sms.md`.

Scenario: a customer in the founding zone texts "water coming through the kitchen ceiling" at 1:10 a.m.

---

**Message 1**
> SOS SMS here. This is an automated reply. To reach a person, [how to reach a person]. For fire, a gas smell, or anyone hurt, call 911 first.

**Message 2**
> To slow the water now: turn off your main water shutoff. [Where it usually is and how to turn it, for a ceiling leak: written and reviewed by a licensed plumber.] Keep clear of wet ceilings and anything electrical near the water.

**Message 3**
> If you want a plumber tonight: we send your request to the licensed, insured plumbers on SOS SMS near you, all at once. The first who can come accepts and texts you an arrival time. The trip charge is [trip charge], credited to the repair if you go ahead. If they are not there within 90 minutes of [clock start], [refund] comes back. Reply YES to agree. We send no payment link until you do.

**Message 4, after YES**
> Thanks. Here is the SOS SMS payment link for the [trip charge] trip charge: [link]. Nothing else is charged now.

**Message 5, on acceptance**
> [Contractor name] is on the way. Arrival time: [time]. Google rating: [rating], where they have one. They get your address now; nobody else does.

---

## Why this works
- **Who before what.** Message 1 names SOS SMS, says it is automated, and gives the safety line before anything else (S72, decision 8). The scam pattern is a link from an unknown number (S59); there is no link until message 4, after agreement.
- **Value before payment.** Message 2 helps whether or not they buy. That is the demand driver.
- **Mechanics, not adjectives.** Who comes, how they are chosen, what it costs, what is credited, what happens if they are late. No "fast", no "best", no average.
- **Brackets stay visible.** Every number and every undecided rule is a bracket. Filling one with a plausible guess would make this a fabricated promise.
- **One idea per message**, short lines, the full name every time.
