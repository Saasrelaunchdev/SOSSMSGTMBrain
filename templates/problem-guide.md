# Template: problem guide (customers)

Pages that answer an urgent question first ("Water leaking through the ceiling: what to do first"), then offer the text button. Problem ad groups land here too. Load `reference/channel-engine-paid-search.md`. Steps cover shutoff and safety only, never repairs, and are reviewed by a licensed plumber before publishing.

## Fields
- Problem (one): burst or leaking pipe / water heater / toilet overflow / sewer backup / ceiling leak
- Search phrases it answers:
- Reviewer (a founding contractor, licensed) and review date:
- Zone, if zone-specific:

## Sections, in order
1. **Safety first.** The safety line. For gas: "Smell gas? Get out first, then call 911 and your gas company."
2. **Do this now.** Numbered shutoff steps for this problem, plainest words, one action per step. Where the main shutoff usually is.
3. **Then this.** Move valuables, photograph the damage for insurance, start drying (EPA: within 24 to 48 hours, S58).
4. **Can it wait until morning?** Plain signs that it can, and signs that it needs someone tonight.
5. **If it needs someone tonight.** The text button. One line on how SOS SMS works, the trip charge stated first, and the guarantee line with its scope or brackets. Only in zones at density.
6. **Before next time.** Text READY.

## Never on a guide
Repair instructions; damage-cost figures without a source; any claim that SOS SMS prevents damage; arrival averages; product claims in the steps themselves.

Guardrails: one side only (customers). Value before any offer. Check `rules/do-not-say.md` and `rules/writing-rules.md`; run `ops/QA-checklist.md`.
