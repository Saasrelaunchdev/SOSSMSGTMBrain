# Template: problem and prevention guides (members)

Pages that answer an urgent or a seasonal question first ("Water leaking through the ceiling: what to do first", "How to prevent frozen pipes"), then offer readiness: the scorecard and the membership, for next time. Load `reference/channel-engine-members.md`. Steps cover shutoff and safety only, never repairs, and are reviewed by a licensed plumber before publishing.

## Fields
- Guide type: problem / prevention
- Problem or season (one): burst or leaking pipe / water heater / toilet overflow / sewer backup / ceiling leak / first freeze
- Search phrases it answers:
- Reviewer (a founding contractor, licensed) and review date:
- Zone, if zone-specific:

## Sections, in order
1. **Safety first.** The safety line. For gas: "Smell gas? Get out first, then call 911 and your gas company."
2. **Do this now** (problem guides) or **Do this before** (prevention guides). Numbered shutoff or prevention steps, plainest words, one action per step. Where the main shutoff usually is.
3. **Then this.** Move valuables, photograph the damage for insurance, start drying (EPA: within 24 to 48 hours, S58).
4. **Can it wait until morning?** Plain signs that it can, and signs that it needs someone tonight.
5. **Ready for next time.** The readiness scorecard; how membership works in one text, with the ZIP check. A reader in the middle of an emergency gets the steps and the safety line first, never a sign-up first, and is never promised dispatch they cannot get as a non-member.
6. **Text READY** for the home check.

## Never on a guide
Repair instructions; damage-cost figures without a source; any claim that SOS SMS prevents damage; arrival averages; product claims in the steps themselves; a promise of dispatch to a non-member.

Guardrails: one side only (members). Value before any offer. Check `rules/do-not-say.md` and `rules/writing-rules.md`; run `ops/QA-checklist.md`.
