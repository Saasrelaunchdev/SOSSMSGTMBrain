# Template: problem and prevention guides (members)

Pages that answer an urgent or a seasonal question first ("Water leaking through the ceiling: what to do first", "No heat on a cold night: what to check first", "A breaker that will not reset", "How to prevent frozen pipes"), then offer readiness: the scorecard and the membership, for next time. Load `reference/channel-engine-members.md`. Steps cover shutoff and safety only, never repairs (for HVAC and electrical, the safety steps only), and are reviewed by a licensed contractor in the guide's trade before publishing.

## Sections, in order
1. **Safety first.** The safety line. For gas: "Smell gas? Get out first, then call 911 and your gas company." Sparks, a burning smell, smoke, or a shock: the safety line first, before any step (decision 8).
2. **Do this now** (problem guides) or **Do this before** (prevention guides). Numbered shutoff, safety, or prevention steps, plainest words, one action per step. Where the main water shutoff or the breaker panel usually is.
3. **Then this** (water problems). Move valuables, photograph the damage for insurance, start drying (EPA: within 24 to 48 hours, S58).
4. **Can it wait until morning?** Plain signs that it can, and signs that it needs someone tonight.
5. **Ready for next time.** The readiness scorecard; how membership works in one text, with the ZIP check. A reader in the middle of an emergency gets the steps and the safety line first, never a sign-up first, and is never promised dispatch they cannot get as a non-member.
6. **Text READY** for the home check.

## Never on a guide
Repair instructions; damage-cost figures without a source; any claim that SOS SMS prevents damage; arrival averages; product claims in the steps themselves; a promise of dispatch to a non-member.

Guardrails: one side only (members). Value before any offer. Check `rules/do-not-say.md` and `rules/writing-rules.md`; run `ops/QA-checklist.md`.
