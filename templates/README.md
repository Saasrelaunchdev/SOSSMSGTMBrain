# Templates

Shapes to fill, one per channel in the SOS SMS mix and no others. Every template is for one side only. Every template ends with the guardrail line; it is where a hurried operator stops reading, so keep it.

| Template | Side | Channel | Engine |
|---|---|---|---|
| `affiliate-kit.md` | Members | Affiliates, before the need | `reference/channel-engine-members.md` |
| `member-email.md` | Members | Email: the launch list, the opening, welcome, seasonal, renewal | `reference/channel-engine-members.md` |
| `membership-page.md` | Members | The membership page and zone pages | `reference/channel-engine-members.md` |
| `problem-guide.md` | Members | Problem and prevention guides, for plumbing, HVAC, and electrical | `reference/channel-engine-members.md` |
| `search-ad.md` | Members | Sign-up ads for paid social at launch, on the open zone's map. Its search ad drafts are held: no paid search at launch (decision 11); a brand campaign may run from Stage 3, once search volume for SOS SMS by name is counted (R68) | `reference/channel-engine-members.md` |
| `first-reply-sms.md` | Members | The welcome texts, the first reply, transactional texts, the reply to a non-member | `reference/channel-engine-members.md` |
| `cold-email.md` | Contractors | Cold email, four over twelve days | `reference/channel-engine-contractors.md` |
| `partner-kit.md` | Contractors | Partnership program materials | `reference/channel-engine-contractors.md` |
| `terms-on-one-page.md` | Contractors | The terms on one page | `reference/channel-engine-contractors.md` |

No template exists for sales scripts, demos, door-to-door, LinkedIn, cold email or texts to homeowners, ads that sell dispatch during an emergency, or Local Services Ads. Those channels are out of the mix; refuse them (`rules/writing-rules.md`).
