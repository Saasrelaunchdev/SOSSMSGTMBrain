# Templates

Shapes to fill, one per channel in the SOS SMS mix and no others. Every template is for one side only. Every template ends with the guardrail line; it is where a hurried operator stops reading, so keep it.

| Template | Side | Channel | Engine |
|---|---|---|---|
| `search-ad.md` | Customers | Paid search, Google then Microsoft | `reference/channel-engine-paid-search.md` |
| `landing-page-zone.md` | Customers | Zone landing pages and zone pages | `reference/channel-engine-paid-search.md` |
| `problem-guide.md` | Customers | Problem-solving pages | `reference/channel-engine-paid-search.md` |
| `first-reply-sms.md` | Customers | The first reply and transactional texts | `reference/channel-engine-paid-search.md` |
| `cold-email.md` | Contractors | Cold email, four over twelve days | `reference/channel-engine-contractors.md` |
| `partner-kit.md` | Contractors | Partnership program materials | `reference/channel-engine-contractors.md` |
| `terms-on-one-page.md` | Contractors | The terms on one page | `reference/channel-engine-contractors.md` |

No template exists for sales scripts, demos, LinkedIn, social posts, outbound homeowner email or texts, or Local Services Ads. Those channels are out of the mix; refuse them (`rules/writing-rules.md`).
