# Template: the membership page (members)

The page every member channel lands on: affiliate links (with the partner's code), the opening email, guides, and paid social sign-up ads; and the organic zone page ("Plumbing, HVAC, and electrical emergency membership in {{zone name}}"). One template, one per zone. Load `reference/channel-engine-members.md` and `reference/value-proposition-members.md`. Example: `examples/membership-page.md`. The words for the plans come from the pricing handoff's membership page table (`sources/raw/gtm/pricing/pricing-handoff.md`).

## Fields
Merge fields from the zone record: `{{zone name}}`, `{{zone neighborhoods}}`, `{{SOS SMS number}}` (registered, decision 9); from the member record: `{{renewal date}}`; from the affiliate agreement: `{{partner}}`, `{{code}}`. Decided values, written as is (2026-10-21): the three plans, Basic $100 a year or $10 a month, Plus $150 or $15, Premier $350 or $35 (R51, R52, R65, R66), and their entitlements (R53, R69); the $99 trip charge on use (decision 5); no fee per request (decision 2); the guarantee scope (decision 3); covered at any hour in an open zone (R15); cancel any time (R56). Never on the page: the plan mix or any contribution figure.

## Sections, in order
1. **Above the fold, on a phone.** "One text, and help is on the way." The ZIP check (open zone: join; zone not yet open: the launch list, no charge, no service promised; outside: the readiness steps alone). The safety line directly under it. On a partner's page, the partner's welcome line and the disclosure.
2. **How it works in one text.** You join once, with your card and home address. When something breaks, plumbing, HVAC, or electrical, you text what happened. The licensed, insured contractors on SOS SMS for that trade see it at once; the first who can come accepts and texts you an arrival time.
3. **The three plans.** Plus in the middle, flagged "Most popular"; the annual plan shown first, with a monthly and annual toggle; "two months free" on every annual plan. Each plan's row from the handoff table: the price, the trades, the phones that can text for your home (your phone, up to 5, up to 10 across two homes), when dispatch starts (annual: from the day you join; monthly: 30 days after), the late-arrival credit (not included, 6 months, 12 months), and on Premier a second home and a trip charge a membership year paid by SOS SMS. The plans never differ by how fast a request goes out. Then, on its own line: the $99 trip charge on use, on your card when a contractor accepts, credited to the repair. No fee per request. Never one total. Nothing about the repair price.
4. **The 90-minute guarantee, Plus and Premier.** "If the contractor is late: the trip charge pays for the work, and we credit the membership you paid for the 6 months (Premier: 12 months) before your request to your next bills." On every plan, in the handoff's words: no acceptance, "You pay nothing, and we tell you within 15 minutes"; after 90 minutes, text NEXT; a contractor who accepts and does not come, the trip charge back in full. Covered: any hour, anywhere in `{{zone neighborhoods}}`. Never a guarantee on Basic. Link to the guarantee in full.
5. **What is included, and what is not.** The handoff's "What is included" table. Not included on any plan: the repair, parts, a guarantee on the work, or faster dispatch (R53). A warranty pays for covered repairs; SOS SMS gets someone there.
6. **Cancel and change plans any time.** On this page, or by texting HELP to reach a person. Annual ends at the end of the paid year, monthly at the end of the paid month; annual members get a reminder before `{{renewal date}}` (R56). Upgrades and downgrades as R54 words them. Stated next to the price, before the card (S87).
7. **Who SOS SMS is.** SOS SMS dispatches licensed, insured contractors; the contractor does the work. The license for each trade, and insurance, checked before they can take a job (decision 7).
8. **Your information.** Only the contractor who takes your job gets your address (decision 4). No selling, no ad targeting.
9. **Join.** The plan, the card (with an optional backup card), the home address (two on Premier), the phones that can text for the home, consent to the member terms (R54, R69). Then the welcome texts.
10. **Footer.** Covered areas, any hour; links to how it works, the guarantee, the member terms, privacy.

## Technical
Loads fast on a phone; the ZIP check and join above the fold; no ad pixels or retargeting; no form that collects a phone number for marketing. Outside an open zone, the page says plainly that SOS SMS does not cover that area yet.

Guardrails: one side only (members). The safety line stays still and legible. Check `rules/do-not-say.md`, `rules/feature-status.md`, `rules/glossary.md`; run `ops/QA-checklist.md`.
