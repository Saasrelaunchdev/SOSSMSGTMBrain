## The five blocks, in this order, plain words

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| What you pay | [Revenue model]. No subscription. No per-lead fee. Any future fee: per completed paid job only, never per request, announced in the agreement |
| When you are paid | The customer pays the trip charge before you roll. SOS SMS holds it and releases it when you arrive. It reaches your bank [payout timing] |
| How requests work | Every matched contractor sees the request at once; the first to accept gets it. You see the problem, any photos, and the approximate distance; the address after you accept. Decline anything; declining does not change which requests reach you |
| What you commit to | An arrival time when you accept, inside the remaining window. Crediting the trip charge to the work if the customer goes ahead. Current license and insurance [recheck cadence]. Your price for the work stays yours; the trip charge is [set by SOS SMS per trade and zone] |
| If you are late | [Late-arrival rule]. Recommended: if you miss the time you committed to, the customer's refund comes from your trip charge; if the miss is caused by SOS SMS, SOS SMS pays |
