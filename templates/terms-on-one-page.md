## The five blocks, in this order, plain words

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| What you pay | $0 to join and $0 per request seen or declined. No subscription or seat fee. No per-lead fee. $25 per completed paid job, from the first job, deducted from the payout: $74 of each $99 trip charge reaches you. One set of terms for every contractor in every trade; they never depend on the member's plan |
| When you are paid | The trip charge goes on the SOS SMS member's card when you accept, before you roll. SOS SMS holds it and releases it when you arrive. It reaches your bank within 2 business days of arrival |
| How requests work | Requests match the trades (plumbing, HVAC, electrical) and area you set. Every matched contractor sees the request at once; the first to accept gets it. You see the problem, any photos, and the approximate distance; the address after you accept. Each request stays open to you for 10 minutes, in the hours you mark available. Decline anything; declining does not change which requests reach you |
| What you commit to | An arrival time when you accept, inside the remaining window. Crediting the trip charge to the work if the member goes ahead. A current license for each trade you take, and insurance, checked at sign-up, every 6 months, and on each policy's expiry date. Your price for the work stays yours; the trip charge is set by SOS SMS, one per trade and zone: $99 in each of plumbing, HVAC, and electrical |
| If you are late | If you arrive after the time you gave, the trip charge still pays for the work, less the fee, and the late arrival is recorded. If you accept and do not come, the trip charge goes back to the member and you are paid nothing. Two late arrivals or no-shows in a rolling 90 days pause your requests until a person from SOS SMS reviews them (R67) |
