## The five blocks, in this order, plain words

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| What you pay | $0 to join and $0 per request seen or declined. No subscription. No per-lead fee. $0 a job in the founding period; then $25 per completed paid job, deducted from the payout. Founding contractors: $0 a job for 12 months after the founding period |
| When you are paid | The trip charge goes on the SOS SMS member's card when you accept, before you roll. SOS SMS holds it and releases it when you arrive. It reaches your bank within 2 business days of arrival |
| How requests work | Every matched contractor sees the request at once; the first to accept gets it. You see the problem, any photos, and the approximate distance; the address after you accept. Each request stays open to you for 10 minutes, in the hours you mark available. Decline anything; declining does not change which requests reach you |
| What you commit to | An arrival time when you accept, inside the remaining window. Crediting the trip charge to the work if the member goes ahead. Current license and insurance, checked at sign-up, every 6 months, and on each policy's expiry date. Your price for the work stays yours; the trip charge is set by SOS SMS, one per trade and zone: $99 for plumbing |
| If you are late | If you miss the time you committed to, the member's refund comes from your trip charge. If SOS SMS caused the miss, SOS SMS pays |
