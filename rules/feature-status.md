# Feature status ledger

Governs what can be promised. Update this file the moment status changes, before any copy follows.
last_reviewed: 2026-10-02

Legend:
- SHIPPED: live now, claimable in the present tense. **Nothing is SHIPPED.**
- IN BUILD: committed and being built. Mark "coming at launch" or omit.
- PLANNED: in the product design; build status unconfirmed. Copy may describe it for launch day only, held until it ships.
- DESIGN OPEN: the product decision is not made. Copy keeps its [bracket].
- CANDIDATE: proposed by research, not in the product. Out of copy.
- NOT BUILT: out of every piece of copy.
- FLAG ONLY: an idea recorded for later. Out of copy.

Source: the product features matrix, both runs' Phase 1, and the cross-side check. Product build status is unconfirmed (open, owned by the product team).
