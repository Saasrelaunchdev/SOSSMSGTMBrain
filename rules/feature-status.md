## Member side
Revised 2026-10-11, the membership revision: the demand side is a membership, joined before the need. Revised 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-21: three trades in one membership, three plans, the late-arrival credit on Plus and Premier.

- Three plans, monthly or annual: PLANNED. Billed to the card on file. Basic $10 a month or $100 a year; Plus $15 or $150; Premier $35 or $350; two months free on every annual plan (decision 2; R51, R52, R65, R66). The prices are decided and go into copy as written. The membership page shows Plus first, in the middle, on the annual plan, flagged "Most popular".
- Plumbing, HVAC, and electrical in one membership: PLANNED. Burst pipes, leaks, backups, and water heaters; no heat and no cooling; a dead panel or circuit (decision 1).
- Joining and changing plans: PLANNED. On the membership page, or from Text READY; the plan, the card (with an optional backup card), the home address (two on Premier), the phones on file, and consent to the member terms; the contact card sent at once. Dispatch starts 30 days after joining on the monthly plan, at once on the annual plan. Upgrades at once on annual, 30 days after on monthly; downgrades at the end of the paid period (R54).
- Phones that can text for the home: PLANNED. Basic, the member's phone; Plus, up to 5; Premier, up to 10 across two homes. A text from any phone on file starts dispatch (R69).
- Memberships open per zone: PLANNED. Only once each of the three trades meets the density number in every hour band, every day; before then, the launch list: email, no charge, no service promised (R55). An open zone is covered at every hour (R15).
- Cancelling, renewal, and failed payments: PLANNED. Cancel any time on the membership page, or by texting HELP to reach a person; monthly ends at the end of the paid month, annual at the end of the paid year, no partial refunds; annual members get a renewal reminder before `{{renewal date}}`; a failed payment is retried for 7 days, then the membership pauses (R56).
- Welcome texts: PLANNED. The plan, when dispatch starts, the contact card, how it works in one text, the safety line, text HELP for a person.
- One number to text: PLANNED. One local number in the launch metro's area code, registered for A2P 10DLC (decision 9).
- One request text starts dispatch: PLANNED. The card and address are on file, so the member's one text is the whole request: no forms, no payment entry, nothing to confirm. The same dispatch on every plan.
- Broadcast to matched contractors, all at once, first to accept: PLANNED. The first to accept with an arrival time inside the remaining window gets the job (decision 4).
- Arrival time texted on acceptance, with the contractor's name: PLANNED.
- The 90-minute guarantee, Plus and Premier: PLANNED. When a contractor arrives after the 90 minutes, does not come, or the member texts NEXT after them, SOS SMS credits the membership paid in the 6 months (Plus: up to $90 monthly, $75 annual) or 12 months (Premier: up to $420 monthly, $350 annual) before the request to the next bills or the renewal; never cash (decision 3, R65, R66). The 90 minutes start at the request text. Basic has no credit.
- Trip charge on use, credited to the repair: PLANNED. $99, one per trade and zone, the same in all three trades, day or night, set by SOS SMS (decision 5); stated on the membership page before joining; charged to the card on file when a contractor accepts (decision 6). It stands on a late arrival and comes back in full on a no-show. No fee per request (decision 2).
- Premier's trip charge a year: PLANNED. One trip charge a membership year paid by SOS SMS: on the annual plan from joining, on the monthly plan after 6 paid months (R66).
- Premier's second home: PLANNED. A second home in an open zone (R66).
- No acceptance: PLANNED. The member is told within 15 minutes and is never charged; no contractor is paid (R11).
- NEXT after the 90 minutes: PLANNED. The first trip charge comes back in full, that contractor is paid nothing, and the request goes to the other matched contractors at once; a new acceptance is a new request with its own $99 and its own 90 minutes (R17).
- A declined card: PLANNED. The backup card is tried at once; if both decline, the member is told at once and dispatch starts the moment a card authorizes (R16).
- A text from a non-member, or from a phone not on file: PLANNED. The safety line, the first steps for the problem, and how to join; dispatch is for members (R57).
- Contractor's Google rating sent with acceptance, where they have one: PLANNED.
- First reply with the first steps for the problem: PLANNED.
- Safety triage (gas, smoke, fire, sparks, a burning smell, a shock, or injury): PLANNED. The safety reply goes first, and nothing is broadcast until the member confirms they are safe and the utility or 911 has been called (decision 8).
- Readiness kit (the shutoff guide, Text READY, the contact card), a readiness check by text once a year, forwardable card: PLANNED (R53).
- Late-arrival credit text: PLANNED. States `{{credit amount}}` and the bills it goes to.
- Member identity hidden until acceptance: PLANNED. Contractors see the problem, any photos or video, and an approximate distance and cross-street area; the address on file goes only to the contractor who accepts (decision 4).
- Affiliate program (a code and a disclosed commission per new paid member): PLANNED. $20 on any plan, paid once the member's second monthly payment or first annual payment has cleared (R61); never to anyone referring at a home purchase settlement.
- Priority or faster dispatch on any plan: NOT AT LAUNCH. Every plan gets the same dispatch (R53).
- Price range before arrival: NOT AT LAUNCH (R49). The $99 trip charge is stated on the membership page instead.
- Repair coverage, parts, or discounts on repairs: NOT AT LAUNCH. No plan pays for repairs (R21, R53).
- Member referral reward: NOT AT LAUNCH. Members are thanked by text (R62).
- Guarantee on the repair: NOT AT LAUNCH. The guarantee covers the arrival window only (R21).
- Paid search: NOT AT LAUNCH. No Google search ads, no Microsoft Ads, never Local Services Ads (decision 11). A brand campaign may run from Stage 3, once search volume for SOS SMS by name is counted (R68).

## Contractor side
- Sign-up by text (JOIN), documents, payout setup, agreement: PLANNED. About 5 minutes by text (R36).
- License and insurance checks before the first request: PLANNED. The state license for each trade a contractor takes (plumbing, HVAC, electrical), and insurance; rechecked every 6 months and on each policy's expiry date (decision 7).
- Accept or decline freely, without changing which requests arrive: PLANNED.
- Response window: PLANNED. 10 minutes, when marked available (R12).
- Zero cost to join: PLANNED. $0 to join, $0 per request seen or declined, no subscription or seat fee (decision 2).
- Contractor fee: PLANNED. $25 per completed paid job, from the first job, for every contractor in every trade, deducted from the trip charge payout: $74 of each $99 to the contractor (decision 2, R03). No founding period, terms, or list (R05 to R07, retired). The contractor's terms never depend on the member's plan.
- Late arrivals and no-shows on the contractor's record: PLANNED. A late contractor keeps the trip charge less the fee; a contractor who does not come is paid nothing; two in a rolling 90 days pause requests until a person from SOS SMS reviews them (R67).
- Trip charge collected before the contractor rolls, released on arrival: PLANNED. Stripe Connect: authorized on the member's card on file when the request text arrives, captured at acceptance, held, released on arrival (decision 6). Premier's trip charge a year is paid by SOS SMS, so the contractor is paid the same.
- Payout timing: PLANNED. Within 2 business days of arrival (decision 6, R14).
- Add-a-technician flow: PLANNED.
- Fifth-trip referral prompt: PLANNED. No referral reward at launch; contractors are thanked by text (R18).
- Lead Cost Check calculator: PLANNED.
- Photos or video before accepting: PLANNED, when the member sends them (decision 4).
- Accepting requests by reply text, no app: PLANNED.
- First refusal on a contractor's own customers: retired (R13). Every request goes to all matched contractors at once; a contractor may refer their own customers through the affiliate program on the same terms as any partner (R61).
- Paid priority for contractors: NOT AT LAUNCH.
