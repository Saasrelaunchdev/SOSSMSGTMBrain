# Do not say

The claim hygiene spine. Check every claim here before it ships. Left column is forbidden or risky, right column is the safe replacement. Decision numbers refer to the twelve in `ops/decisions.md`.
last_reviewed: 2026-10-02

## Hard bans

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| Any capability in the present tense as if live ("text us now and a plumber is on the way") | Launch-day copy, held until it ships, per `feature-status.md`. In any preview: "Pre-launch. Not live." |
| Any number not in the brain: counts, prices, fees, arrival averages, results, savings | `{TBD}` for unknowns. Sourced benchmarks only with their source ID, never as SOS SMS results |
| Testimonials, ratings, logos, contractor or customer counts | Nothing. The proof-point set in `ops/copy-bank.md` is empty |
| A filled [bracket] or a guessed {TBD} | The bracket or {TBD}, visible, until `ops/decisions.md` logs the decision |
| "Escrow", "held in escrow" | "SOS SMS holds the trip charge until you arrive", plus the release rule once decision 6 is made |
| "Exclusive requests", "exclusive leads" | "Every matched contractor sees the request at once. The first to accept gets it. Losing one costs nothing" |
| "Pre-qualified", "ready-to-buy customers" | "Matched to the trades and service area you set"; "people with an active problem who want someone now" |
| "Guaranteed arrival time" | "An arrival time when they accept", and separately the 90-minute window with its scope |
| The guarantee without its scope | "If they are not there within 90 minutes of [clock start], [refund] comes back", covered areas and hours named, once decision 3 is made |
| "Relaunch guarantee", "we'll send another" | Nothing. NOT BUILT, out of copy |
| A guarantee on the repair or the workmanship | "We check license and insurance before they can take a job." The work is the contractor's |
| SOS SMS as the plumber, an emergency service, or 911 | "SOS SMS dispatches licensed, insured contractors. The contractor does the work" |
| An urgent call to text without the safety line | The safety line, every time (`glossary.md`) |
| "More jobs", "more revenue", "earn more", any volume promise | "No charge to see a request. The trip is paid before you roll" |
| "Damage prevented", "save your home" | "Stop-the-water steps in the first reply" |
| "Every contractor in your area" | "The vetted contractors on SOS SMS for your trade", only in zones at density |
| "24/7" or "any time" | The covered hours of that zone, named |
| "Zero cost, only we can do this" | "No subscription and no per-lead fees", with how SOS SMS is paid in the same sentence once decision 2 is made |
| "Your trip charge" to a contractor | "Your price for the work stays yours" (decision 5) |
| Copy that blends contractors and customers | Pick one side. Two assets |
| Copy for an excluded channel | The refusal and the redirect in `writing-rules.md`, never a softer version |

## In search ads, by text, and by email
- **Search ads.** No superlatives, no arrival averages, no "24/7" unless the zone's covered hours are. The guarantee appears only once decision 3 is made, with its scope on the landing page. The safety line on every landing page and in one ad description. No bids on "SOS plumbing" until decision 10 is done. Never Local Services Ads (S70).
- **Text (SMS).** The first reply says it is automated, who SOS SMS is, the safety line, the trip charge, and the guarantee scope before any link (S72, S59). No marketing texts without prior written consent (S31). No link in a message that has not first said who is sending it.
- **Email.** Accurate sender, honest subject line, the postal address, a one-click opt-out honored within 10 business days (S73). Contractor email 1 under 120 words. No claims about SOS SMS results. Homeowners get email only after opting in on the readiness scorecard; never cold email a homeowner.

## Claims that need scoping
- **"Zero cost to join. Only we can do this."** Counter-example: free sign-up is common, and warranty networks charge no join fee (S33). Safe form: "no subscription and no per-lead fees" plus how SOS SMS is paid.
- **"Text-only access, only we can do this."** Counter-example: plumbing shops already take customer texts through two-way texting tools (S64). Safe form: a mechanic, "works entirely by text", never "only".
- **"Pre-qualified leads."** Counter-example: the FTC found a lead platform's quality claims unsubstantiated (S01). Safe form: "matched to the trades and area you set".
- **"High-urgency, ready to buy."** Counter-example: some customers decline the quote. Safe form: "people with an active problem who want someone now".
- **"Private by design, never shared."** Counter-example: the accepting contractor gets the address. Safe form: "only the contractor who takes your job gets your address and details", once conflict C is resolved.
- **"Verified, so you know they're qualified."** Counter-example: a license check does not test workmanship. Safe form: "we check license and insurance before they can take a job".
- **"Guaranteed arrival."** Counter-example: Benjamin Franklin Plumbing already guarantees punctuality for booked appointments. Safe form: the 90-minute window with clock start, refund, and covered areas and hours; never "the only guarantee".
- **"The only one that pays you to show up."** Counter-example: warranty networks pay for dispatched visits, at their set rates (S20, S33). Holds only against lead platforms (Angi, Thumbtack, Google LSA, Service Direct). Safe form: "lead platforms charge you to look; SOS SMS collects the trip charge before you roll".

## Historical and reputational material
- **HomeAdvisor and the FTC (S01, S02).** A 2023 final order of up to $7.2 million over unsubstantiated lead-quality claims, and over $3 million returned to businesses. Use it only to explain why SOS SMS does not claim lead quality. Never name a rival as a fraud.
- **Google badges (S09).** Since 2025-10-20 Google Guaranteed, Google Screened, and License Verified are one Google Verified badge, and the Money Back Guarantee is gone. Never cite the old badges as current.
- **The name collision.** A 2026-10-01 search for "SOS SMS" returned unrelated local plumbing companies with "SOS" in their names. Always the full name; never imply a link to them.
- **Text scams (S59).** $470 million lost in 2024 to scams that started with a text. Handle it by design (who we are before any link), never by fear.
