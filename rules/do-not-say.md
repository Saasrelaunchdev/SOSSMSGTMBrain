# Do not say

The claim hygiene spine. Check every claim here before it ships. Left column is forbidden or risky, right column is the safe replacement. Decision numbers refer to the twelve in `ops/decisions.md`, decided 2026-10-03 and revised by the membership revision on 2026-10-11; R numbers to `reference/resolution-register.md`.
last_reviewed: 2026-10-11

## Hard bans

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| Any capability in the present tense as if live (a page that says "text us now" before memberships open in its zone) | Launch-day copy, held until it ships, per `feature-status.md`. In any preview: "Pre-launch. Not live." The brand promise, "One text, and help is on the way.", runs only where memberships are open, and always with the safety line |
| Any number not in the brain: counts, prices, fees, arrival averages, results, savings | The decided value or planning value from the register, labelled as what it is. A fact that cannot exist before launch is not written: say how and when it is counted. Sourced benchmarks only with their source ID, never as SOS SMS results |
| Testimonials, ratings, logos, contractor or member counts | The drill's method line and its named record (R44, R45). No result exists before launch |
| A guessed number, a placeholder figure, or "coming soon" | The value from `reference/resolution-register.md`, or a `{{merge field}}` for anything that differs per zone, contractor, or message |
| "Escrow", "held in escrow" | "SOS SMS holds the trip charge until you arrive, then releases it to you" (decision 6) |
| "Exclusive requests", "exclusive leads" | "Every matched contractor sees the request at once. The first to accept gets it. Losing one costs nothing" |
| "Pre-qualified", "ready-to-buy customers" | "Matched to the trades and service area you set"; "SOS SMS members with an active problem who want someone now" |
| "Guaranteed arrival time" | "An arrival time when they accept", and separately the 90-minute window with its scope |
| The guarantee without its scope | "If they are not there within 90 minutes of your text, the $99 trip charge comes back", with the covered areas and hours named: `{{zone neighborhoods}}`, `{{covered hours}}` (decision 3) |
| "Relaunch guarantee", "we'll send another" | Nothing. HELD out of copy until dispatch supports it (R17) |
| A guarantee on the repair or the workmanship | "We check license and insurance before they can take a job." The work is the contractor's |
| SOS SMS as the plumber, an emergency service, or 911 | "SOS SMS dispatches licensed, insured contractors. The contractor does the work" |
| An urgent call to text without the safety line | The safety line, every time (`glossary.md`) |
| "More jobs", "more revenue", "earn more", any volume promise | "No charge to see a request. The trip is paid before you roll" |
| "Damage prevented", "save your home", "pays for itself" | "Stop-the-water steps in the first reply"; what the membership includes, and the trip charge only on use |
| "Every contractor in your area" | "The vetted contractors on SOS SMS for your trade", only in zones at density |
| "24/7" or "any time" | The covered hours of that zone, named |
| A total that merges the membership and the trip charge into one number | Two lines, named separately: `{{monthly price}}` or `{{annual price}}` for the membership, and the $99 trip charge on use, credited to the repair (decisions 2 and 5) |
| A planning price as the price ("$10 a month") | `{{monthly price}}` and `{{annual price}}` until the launch price is set in Stage 0 (R51, R52) |
| "Covers repairs", "warranty", "protection plan", "insurance", "members save" | "One text, an arrival time when a contractor accepts, and the 90-minute guarantee. The repair is the contractor's, priced on site" (R21, R53) |
| "Free trial", "first month free" | Nothing: not offered. Cancel any time is the reassurance (R56) |
| Cancellation made hard, or renewal without a reminder | "Cancel any time, on the membership page or by texting HELP"; annual members get a reminder before `{{renewal date}}` (R56, S87) |
| An affiliate mention without the disclosure, or any payment to someone referring at a home purchase settlement | The affiliate's disclosure next to the mention (S88). Agents, lenders, title and settlement agents, and inspectors may share SOS SMS without payment (R61, S86, S94) |
| A dispatch ad aimed at someone in the middle of an emergency ("Pipe burst? Text us now") | Ads sell the membership before the need: "Join before your pipes burst" (decision 11) |
| "Zero cost, only we can do this" | "No subscription and no per-lead fees", with how SOS SMS is paid in the same sentence: by members' monthly or annual fees, and by you only per completed paid job after the founding period, $25 (decision 2) |
| "Your trip charge" to a contractor | "Your price for the work stays yours" (decision 5) |
| Copy that blends contractors and members | Pick one side. Two assets |
| Copy for an excluded channel | The refusal and the redirect in `writing-rules.md`, never a softer version |

## In search ads, by text, and by email
- **Search and social ads.** They sell memberships before the need, in open zones; never dispatch during an emergency (decision 11). No superlatives, no arrival averages, no "24/7" unless the zone's covered hours are. The guarantee appears with its scope on the membership page (decision 3). The safety line on the membership page and in one ad description. Location targeting only: no pixels, retargeting, or uploaded lists. No bids on "SOS plumbing" (decision 10). Never Local Services Ads (S70).
- **Text (SMS).** The first reply to a member's request text says it is automated, that the request is going to the contractors on SOS SMS now, the safety line, and how to reach a person (text HELP); no offer, no link, nothing to confirm (S72, S59). A text from a non-member gets the safety line, the steps, and how to join (R57). No marketing texts without prior written consent (S31). No link in a message that has not first said who is sending it.
- **Email.** Accurate sender, honest subject line, the postal address, a one-click opt-out honored within 10 business days (S73). Contractor email 1 under 120 words. No claims about SOS SMS results. Homeowners get email from SOS SMS only after opting in (the readiness scorecard, the launch list, joining) or through a partner's own list in the partner's send; never cold email a homeowner.
- **Affiliates.** The kit's words only, a code in every link, and the disclosure next to every mention (S88). Never "warranty", "covers repairs", or "insurance".

## Claims that need scoping
- **"Zero cost to join. Only we can do this."** Counter-example: free sign-up is common, and warranty networks charge no join fee (S33). Safe form: "no subscription and no per-lead fees" plus how SOS SMS is paid (decision 2).
- **"Text-only access, only we can do this."** Counter-example: plumbing shops already take customer texts through two-way texting tools (S64). Safe form: a mechanic, "works entirely by text", never "only".
- **"Pre-qualified leads."** Counter-example: the FTC found a lead platform's quality claims unsubstantiated (S01). Safe form: "matched to the trades and area you set".
- **"High-urgency, ready to buy."** Counter-example: some members decline the quote. Safe form: "SOS SMS members with an active problem who want someone now".
- **"Private by design, never shared."** Counter-example: the accepting contractor gets the address on file. Safe form: "only the contractor who takes your job gets your address and details" (decision 4).
- **"Like a warranty, but faster."** Counter-example: a warranty pays for covered repairs; SOS SMS pays for none (R21). Safe form: "a warranty pays for a covered repair; SOS SMS gets a vetted contractor to your door with one text". Many members will hold both.
- **"Priority service."** Counter-example: a plumber's club gives priority with one company (S75). Safe form: "one text reaches every vetted contractor on SOS SMS for your trade at once".
- **"Verified, so you know they're qualified."** Counter-example: a license check does not test workmanship. Safe form: "we check license and insurance before they can take a job".
- **"Guaranteed arrival."** Counter-example: Benjamin Franklin Plumbing already guarantees punctuality for booked appointments. Safe form: the 90-minute window with clock start, refund, and covered areas and hours; never "the only guarantee".
- **"The only one that pays you to show up."** Counter-example: warranty networks pay for dispatched visits, at their set rates (S20, S33). Holds only against lead platforms (Angi, Thumbtack, Google LSA, Service Direct). Safe form: "lead platforms charge you to look; SOS SMS collects the trip charge before you roll".

## Historical and reputational material
- **HomeAdvisor and the FTC (S01, S02).** A 2023 final order of up to $7.2 million over unsubstantiated lead-quality claims, and over $3 million returned to businesses. Use it only to explain why SOS SMS does not claim lead quality. Never name a rival as a fraud.
- **Google badges (S09).** Since 2025-10-20 Google Guaranteed, Google Screened, and License Verified are one Google Verified badge, and the Money Back Guarantee is gone. Never cite the old badges as current.
- **The name collision.** A 2026-10-01 search for "SOS SMS" returned unrelated local plumbing companies with "SOS" in their names. Always the full name; never imply a link to them.
- **Text scams (S59).** $470 million lost in 2024 to scams that started with a text. Handle it by design (who we are before any link), never by fear.
