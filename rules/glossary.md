# Glossary, canonical vocabulary

The words SOS SMS uses for its own things. Mirror them exactly in every asset, on both sides of the marketplace.
last_reviewed: 2026-10-02

## Canonical terms

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| SOS SMS | Always the full name, two words, both in capitals. Never the first word alone: unrelated local plumbing companies use it (open decision 10) |
| contractor | The licensed, insured tradesperson or shop on the supply side. Not "pro", "partner", "vendor", or "provider" in copy |
| customer | The person at home with the problem, on the demand side. Not "user", "client", or "lead" |
| request | What a contractor receives when a customer texts. Never "lead": contractors associate leads with paying to look |
| accept | What a contractor does to take a request. The first to accept gets the job |
| dispatch | What SOS SMS does. SOS SMS dispatches; the contractor does the work. SOS SMS is never "the plumber" |
| matched contractors | The contractors on SOS SMS matched to the request by trade and service area |
| trip charge | What the customer pays for the visit, agreed by text before dispatch, credited to the repair if they go ahead. Amount {TBD} |
| SOS SMS fee | The customer-side per-request fee, the recommended home of revenue. Amount {TBD} |
| arrival time | The time a contractor commits to when accepting |
| 90-minute window | The window the guarantee covers, from the clock start in [scope] |
| guarantee | The 90-minute guarantee. Always with clock start, refund, and covered areas and hours in the same sentence |
| zone | The unit of launch: a small area inside one metro |
| hour band | A slice of the day. 2 a.m. and 2 p.m. are different bands for density |
| covered hours | The hour bands in a zone where the density number is met. Ads run only in covered hours |
| density number | Contractors needed per trade, zone, and hour band for a target chance that at least one accepts |
| acceptance rate | The chance one contractor accepts a request in time. {TBD} until measured |
| founding contractor, founding period | The launch cohort in the first zone, and the period in which contractors pay $0 (recommended) |
| owned share | Requests from a saved number, a forwarded card, or an organic page rather than an ad |
| first reply | The automated reply to a customer's first text: who SOS SMS is, the safety line, stop-the-water steps, the trip charge, the guarantee scope, before any link |
| Text READY | The pre-need keyword: a five-question check and a shutoff guide by text, ending with the contact card |
| contact card | The saved contact every customer gets, so the next emergency starts with a saved number |
| forwardable card | The card a customer forwards to a neighbor after a job |
| the terms on one page | The contractor's terms: what they pay, when they are paid, what they commit to, what happens if they are late |
| Lead Cost Check | The contractor-side demand driver: a two-minute calculator of true cost per booked job |
| Job Leak Scorecard | The contractor lead magnet |
| safety line | "For fire, a gas smell, or anyone hurt, call 911 first." Gas variant: "Smell gas? Get out first, then call 911 and your gas company." |
| {TBD} | An unknown. Never filled with a guess, a range, or "coming soon" |
| [bracket] | Copy waiting on a named founder decision. Never filled until the decision is logged |

## Banned near-synonyms

| Reached for | Why it is wrong | Use |
|---|---|---|
| SOS (alone) | Name collision with unrelated plumbing companies | SOS SMS |
| lead, hot lead, qualified lead | Means paying to look; it is the model SOS SMS replaces | request |
| exclusive | Requests go to all matched contractors at once | shared openly, first to accept |
| escrow | A legal term for licensed arrangements | SOS SMS holds the trip charge until you arrive |
| pre-qualified, pre-screened customer | Unsubstantiated quality claim (S01) | matched to the trades and area you set |
| service fee, call-out fee | Blurs the trip charge with the SOS SMS fee | trip charge, or SOS SMS fee, named separately |
| guaranteed arrival time | The arrival time is a commitment; only the window is guaranteed | arrival time, and the 90-minute window |
| emergency service, 24/7 emergency plumber | SOS SMS dispatches; it is not an emergency service | emergency home repair dispatch by text |
| every contractor in your area | Only contractors on SOS SMS, and only at density | the vetted contractors on SOS SMS for your trade |
| Google Guaranteed, Google Screened | Retired by Google on 2025-10-20 (S09), and not ours | license and insurance checked by SOS SMS |
| your trip charge (to contractors) | One trip charge per trade and zone, set by SOS SMS (recommended) | your price for the work |

## Distinctions that must never blur

| Pair | The difference |
|---|---|
| arrival time vs 90-minute window | The contractor commits to an arrival time. SOS SMS guarantees the window. A claim true of one is false of the other |
| trip charge vs SOS SMS fee | The trip charge pays for the visit and is credited to the repair. The SOS SMS fee pays SOS SMS. Never one number for both |
| contractor vs customer | Two audiences, two value propositions, never one asset |
| dispatch vs repair | SOS SMS dispatches. The contractor repairs. There is no guarantee on the work |
| PLANNED vs live | Nothing is live. Copy is written for launch day and held until the capability ships |

The distinction that matters most: **the arrival time is the contractor's promise; the 90-minute window is SOS SMS's promise.** Every guarantee claim hinges on keeping them apart.
