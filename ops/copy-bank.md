# Copy bank

Reusable language. Every line here has passed `rules/do-not-say.md` and carries decided values (2026-10-04; the member lines revised by the membership revision, 2026-10-11; both sides by the tiers revision, 2026-10-20, and the pricing revision, 2026-10-21). SOS SMS is pre-launch and nothing has shipped, so each line carries the condition under which it runs. Use lines verbatim once their condition is met; rewriting a cleared line turns a checked claim into an unchecked one. When a decision changes a claim, search this file for the old phrasing first.

Condition tags: **(now)** usable today, in drafts and in pre-launch contractor recruiting written in the future tense; **(pre-need)** member-side readiness copy that promises no service (the scorecard, prevention guides, the launch list), usable from Stage 1; **(launch day)** membership copy, held until memberships open in the zone; **(at density)** only for open zones, where each of the three trades meets the density number in every hour band (R55); **(when built)** only once the named capability ships. A plan named in the tag (**Basic**, **Plus**, **Premier**, **Plus and Premier**) limits the line to copy about that plan; a line with no plan in its tag holds on every plan.
