Reusable language. Every line here has passed `rules/do-not-say.md` and carries decided values (2026-10-04; the member lines revised by the membership revision, 2026-10-11). SOS SMS is pre-launch and nothing has shipped, so each line carries the condition under which it runs. Use lines verbatim once their condition is met; rewriting a cleared line turns a checked claim into an unchecked one. When a decision changes a claim, search this file for the old phrasing first.

Condition tags: **(now)** usable today, in drafts and in pre-launch contractor recruiting written in the future tense; **(pre-need)** member-side readiness copy that promises no service (the scorecard, prevention guides, the launch list), usable from Stage 1; **(launch day)** membership copy, held until memberships open in the zone; **(at density)** only for open zones, at the density number, in covered hours; **(when built)** only once the named capability ships.
