**Closest rival on the window:** the plumber's or HVAC club. The difference is reach and the clock: a club gives priority with one company, for one or two trades, and its punctuality guarantee runs against a scheduled appointment; the SOS SMS window runs 90 minutes from the member's request text, at any hour in an open zone, across every vetted contractor on SOS SMS for the trade, in three trades. A late arrival keeps its trip charge; on Plus and Premier, 6 or 12 months of membership are credited. On price, Basic sits at the plumber's club price, $10 a month (S75).

**The weak point to own:** a fee every month or year, before anything breaks (R46). Say it first, then what the plans include, the trip charge only on use, and cancel any time.
