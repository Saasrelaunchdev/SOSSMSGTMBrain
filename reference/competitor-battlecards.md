# Competitor battlecards

Load for: sales-free objection replies, comparison pages, partner materials, and any copy that names or implies a rival. Distills both runs' Phase 2 (summary, advantage, vectors; the member slate since 2026-10-11). Never name a rival as dishonest; state mechanics. Grade C figures stay internal.

**Closest rival on the window:** the plumber's club. The difference is reach and the clock: a club gives priority with one company, and its punctuality guarantee runs against a scheduled appointment; the SOS SMS window runs 90 minutes from the member's request text, inside covered hours, across every vetted contractor on SOS SMS for the trade, with the $99 trip charge back on a miss.

**The weak point to own:** a fee every month or year, before anything breaks (R46). Say it first, then what membership includes, the trip charge only on use, and cancel any time.
