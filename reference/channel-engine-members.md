# Channel engine: members (affiliates, email, search and SEO pages, paid social, member loops)

Load for: any affiliate kit, partner send, launch list or member email, membership or zone page, prevention or problem guide, paid social sign-up ad, welcome or first-reply text, and every member template. Distills Run 2 Phase 8 (`phase-8-assets-library.md`, the membership revision, 2026-10-11, and the tiers revision, 2026-10-20). Templates: `templates/affiliate-kit.md`, `templates/member-email.md`, `templates/membership-page.md`, `templates/problem-guide.md`, `templates/search-ad.md` (sign-up ads for paid social at launch; its search drafts are held), `templates/first-reply-sms.md`.

## Thesis
Homeowners join before the need, so SOS SMS meets them where they already trust someone, through partners and email, long before anything breaks. A number saved in a phone before the pipe bursts, the heat fails, or the panel dies is worth more than an ad seen during the emergency. In the middle of an emergency, a homeowner is better served by calling a contractor from the search results than by joining anything, so no ad sells dispatch in an emergency, and there is no paid search at launch (decision 11). No sales team and no calls to households: partners join an affiliate program, and the product does the converting.

## The three channels, plus the loops
1. **Affiliates, before the need (lead).** Utilities and cities (the partner-led model behind more than 1,300 HomeServe partners, S93), homeowner and neighborhood associations, local publications and newsletters, home content creators, home improvement retailers, insurance agents for existing policyholders, employers' benefit platforms. Signed from Stage 0, fastest categories first, codes ready before the zone opens (R25). Each gets the kit, a code, and a disclosed commission: $20 per new paid member on any plan, paid once the member's second monthly payment or first annual payment has cleared (decided, R61). Contractors may join on the same terms.
2. **Email, before the need (lead).** SOS SMS's own homeowner list from the readiness scorecard and the launch list; partner sends to the partner's own list; member email (welcome, seasonal notes with opt-in, the upgrade note, the renewal reminder). CAN-SPAM throughout (S73).
3. **Search and SEO pages and paid social, for sign-ups (support).** Organic: the prevention and problem guides for plumbing, HVAC, and electrical, read before the need; the membership page; zone pages; organic social and community posts by SOS SMS itself. Paid social for sign-ups, limited to the open zone's map, paused with sign-ups (R34), each at or under the ceiling of the plan joined. **No paid search at launch:** no Google search ads, no Microsoft Ads, never Local Services Ads. Held: a brand search campaign from Stage 3, once search volume for SOS SMS by name is counted (R68), at or under the ceiling.
4. **Member loops.** The contact card, Text READY, the forwardable card (no reward at launch, R62), the two questions after a job, upgrades, the renewal.

## Hard rules
- **Zones first.** Memberships open only in zones where each of the three trades meets the density number in every hour band (R55); an open zone is covered every hour of every day (R15). Affiliate links land on the ZIP check: an open zone shows the plans, a zone not yet open the launch list, outside the readiness steps alone. Sign-ups pause automatically when acceptance in any trade falls below 90 percent over 7 days: paid social stops, affiliate links show the launch list (R34).
- **No payment at a home purchase.** Never pay a real estate agent, lender, title or settlement agent, or anyone referring at a home purchase settlement (RESPA Section 8, S86, S94). They may share SOS SMS without payment. Insurance agents are paid only for existing policyholders, outside any settlement.
- **Disclosure.** Every affiliate discloses that they are paid, next to the mention (S88).
- **Sign-ups, never dispatch.** Ads sell the membership before the need; no ad offers dispatch during an emergency. Never Local Services Ads (S70). A brand search campaign, from Stage 3 only (R68), needs Google's written confirmation first (S71), with emergency dispatch searches ("emergency plumber near me", "24 hour plumber") as negative keywords.
- **No retargeting, no pixels.** Location targeting only.
- **Never pose as the contractor, a warranty, or insurance.** SOS SMS is a membership that dispatches licensed, insured plumbers, HVAC technicians, and electricians; the contractor does the work; no plan pays for the repair (R21, R53).
- **Claims.** No superlatives, no arrival averages, never "24/7": an open zone is "any hour, in {{zone neighborhoods}}". The guarantee appears only named with Plus and Premier, with its scope on the membership page (decision 3); never on Basic, and no plan is sold as faster. Prices as decided: Basic $10 a month or $100 a year, Plus $15 or $150, Premier $35 or $350 (R51, R52, R65, R66); the plans, the $99 trip charge, and the safety line on separate lines, never one total. The plan mix and the economics never go on a page.
- **Subscription law.** Clear terms before the card, the 30-day start of dispatch on the monthly plan included (R54), express consent, a renewal reminder before `{{renewal date}}`, and cancel any time, on the membership page or by texting HELP (R56, S87).
- **Safety.** The safety line on the membership page and in one ad description. Texts that mention gas, smoke, fire, sparks, a burning smell, a shock, or injury get the safety reply first (decision 8).
- **Brand terms.** Always the full name; no ad on "SOS" alone, no bids on "SOS plumbing" (decision 10).
- **Optimize for a new paid member**, by plan, never a click, a ZIP check, or a launch list join. Cost per new paid member by channel, plan, and zone, commissions included, against the ceiling of the plan joined, about $47 Basic, $70 Plus, $151 Premier (R63), decides spend.

## Method: plan, write, edit
1. **Plan.** Confirm the zone is open in all three trades, or that the asset only promises the launch list. Pick the channel and its landing page (the membership page with the partner's code, the launch list, or a guide). List the merge fields.
2. **Write.** Ready, not afraid: the gap in the home's readiness, then one text, then the plans and the trip charge on use, then how to cancel. Use the `value-proposition-members.md` drills.
3. **Edit.** Count characters. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md`, the affiliate, email, and ads blocks included.

## Production order
1. Stage 0: the member terms and the automatic renewal disclosures (S87), membership billing for the three plans with upgrades and the late-arrival credit, the affiliate terms (S86, S88, S94), email sending (S73), carrier registration (S32). Without them, nothing sells a membership.
2. The membership page with the three plans, the guarantee in full (Plus and Premier), how it works in one text.
3. In Stage 1, while supply fills all three trades: the readiness scorecard, the first five prevention and problem guides, the launch list sequence.
4. The affiliate program page and the kit; the first partners signed for the launch zone, codes ready before it opens.
5. Member loops: the welcome texts, the first-steps reply, the reply to a non-member, the contact card, the credit and no-show text, READY.
6. When the zone opens: the opening email to the launch list, affiliates live, then paid social for sign-ups. No paid search at launch (decision 11).

## Out, and why
Ads that sell dispatch during an emergency (a contractor from the results is the faster route then); paid search at launch (about $137 per new paid member at the plumbing benchmark, S15, B, about 3 times Basic's ceiling; decision 11); sales calls, demos, meetings, door-to-door; marketing texts without written consent (S31); retargeting and pixels; Local Services Ads (S70); any payment at a home purchase settlement (S86, S94); LinkedIn.
