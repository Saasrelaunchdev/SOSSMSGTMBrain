# Value proposition: members

Load for: any member-facing headline, ad, page, email, affiliate kit, first reply, or transactional text. Distills Run 2 Phase 6 (`phase-6-value-proposition.md`, the membership revision, 2026-10-11). Every social-proof headline is a method line and every proof point a named record (R44, R45): no result exists before launch. No drill states or implies an average arrival time, a count of members or homes helped, a savings figure, repair coverage, or that SOS SMS prevents damage. Every value below is decided or a labelled planning value; per-message details and the membership prices are `{{merge fields}}`.

**Safety line, on every drill that describes the emergency text:** "For fire, a gas smell, or anyone hurt, call 911 first." Gas variant (S60): "Smell gas? Get out first, then call 911 and your gas company." The brand promise always travels with it.

**Brand promise:** One text, and help is on the way. **Sub tag:** Join before you need it.
**Positioning:** The emergency home repair membership you join before anything breaks, so the emergency itself is one text. Built on one message that reaches the vetted contractors for the trade at once, and an arrival window that costs SOS SMS money when it is missed.

## Pillars

| Pillar | Reasons to believe |
|---|---|
| VP1 One text, help on the way | One text reaches the licensed, insured contractors on SOS SMS for that trade nearby, all at once. An arrival time when they accept, with their name and Google rating. If they are not there within 90 minutes of your text, the $99 trip charge comes back, and SOS SMS keeps working to get someone there |
| VP2 Ready before it happens | Your card and home address are on file from the day you join, so there is nothing to fill in at 2 a.m. No app and no phone tree: one number, saved on joining. Monthly or annual, cancel any time, a person by text |
| VP3 Know the cost and who is coming | The $99 trip charge only when a contractor accepts, credited to the repair. License and insurance checked before they can take a job. Only the contractor who takes your job gets your address; no selling, no ad targeting |

## Method substitutes (use in place of social proof)
- "Your text goes to every matched contractor at once. The first who can come accepts." (drill 1)
- "When a contractor accepts, you get their name, their Google rating, and their arrival time by text." (drill 2)
- "The guarantee in one line, on the membership page: 90 minutes from your text, or the trip charge back." (drill 3)
- "You join once, calmly. In an emergency, your one text is the whole request." (drill 4)
- "Join, save one number, and that is the whole setup." (drill 5)
- "What membership includes, what it costs, and how to cancel, on one page before you join." (drill 6)
- "Two prices, both stated before you join: the membership, and the trip charge when you use it." (drill 7)
- "What we check: license, insurance, and when we check again." (drill 8)
- "Contractors see the problem and the neighborhood. The one who accepts gets your address." (drill 9)

## Body lines (25 words max)
- Drill 1: "Text what broke. It reaches the vetted contractors on SOS SMS for that trade nearby, all at once. The first who can come accepts."
- Drill 3: "If your contractor isn't there within 90 minutes of your text, the $99 trip charge comes back, and we keep working. Covered: {{zone neighborhoods}}, {{covered hours}}."
- Drill 4: "You join once, with your card and home address. When something breaks, text what happened. No forms, no payment screen, nothing to spell out."
- Drill 6: "Join once, for {{monthly price}} a month or {{annual price}} a year. Cancel any time, and text HELP to reach a person."
- Drill 7: "The $99 trip charge applies only on use: it goes on your card when a contractor accepts, and is credited to the repair."

Safety line required on drills 1 to 5 and 7; not required on 6, 8, and 9.

## The weak point, said first
"A membership costs something every month whether or not anything breaks" (R46). Answer with what it includes, the trip charge only on use, and cancel any time, before the card.

## Cross-side holds
Drill 3 runs on the decided pair: the fault-based late-arrival rule in the contractor terms and the guarantee scope from the request text (decision 3). Drill 2 needs the contractor's arrival commitment in the terms on one page. Drill 7 needs crediting in the contractor agreement. Drill 8 uses the same recheck cadence on both sides (decision 7). Drills 6 and 7 quote two prices, never one total. "Licensed and insured" is never applied to SOS SMS itself.

## Proof capture
Permission clause in the member terms before the first member joins. After every job, one text: "Did your contractor arrive within the window? Would you tell a neighbor?" Verbatim, dated. Arrival times logged from acceptance to arrival, counted weekly from the first request (R43); joins, cancellations, and renewals logged by channel from the first member. A real figure is cited only with its sample size, its date, and permission.
