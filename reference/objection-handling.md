# Objection handling

Load for: FAQ pages, email replies, partner kit FAQs, the first reply, and any written answer to a pushback. Synthesized from both runs' Phase 5 blockers, Phase 7 key questions, the contractor page FAQ (Phase 8), and the cross-side check. There is no sales team: every answer is written, self-serve, and honest. Brackets stay until their decision is logged.

## Contractors

| Objection | Honest answer | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "What's the catch? How do you make money?" | The customer pays SOS SMS a fee on each request. You pay nothing in the founding period, then $25 per completed paid job, taken from the trip charge payout. No subscription, no per-lead fee | Decision 2 |
| "Every request is shared, like the platforms I quit." | It is shared, openly. Every matched contractor sees it at once and the first to accept gets it. Losing one costs nothing, because nobody pays to look | Decision 4 |
| "I've been burned by lead platforms." | Then read the terms before anything else: what you pay, when you're paid, what you commit to, what happens if you're late, on one page | The terms on one page |
| "When do I actually get the trip charge?" | The customer pays it before you roll; SOS SMS holds it and releases it when you arrive. It reaches your bank within 2 business days | Decisions 3 and 6 |
| "What if I'm late?" | The trip charge is yours when you arrive by the time you committed to. Miss your own time and it goes back to the customer. If SOS SMS caused the miss, SOS SMS pays the refund | Decision 3 |
| "Can I set my own trip charge?" | Your price for the work stays yours. The trip charge is one per trade and zone, set by SOS SMS: $99 for plumbing | Decision 5 |
| "I don't want calls at 2 a.m." | You mark the hours you're available. Decline anything, any time; declining doesn't change which requests reach you | Dispatch never ranks by acceptance |
| "My dispatcher runs the board." (shop) | You choose who receives requests: the dispatcher, the owner, or on-call technicians. Set it before the first request | Add-a-technician flow |
| "Will you steal my customers?" | The customer relationship stays yours. SOS SMS is not offered as your after-hours line until dispatch gives you first refusal on your own customers | R13 |
| "What if the customer disputes?" | Text us. A founder settles it from the request log: the customer's confirming text, your acceptance, your committed arrival time, and your arrival. The rule is in the FAQ before you join | Speed-to-scale plan, operations |
| "Is anyone in my area on it?" | Founding contractors are the first in {{zone}}. Customers open there only when enough contractors have joined to cover it | Never a count |
| "Another app?" | Nothing to download. Requests arrive by text | Sign-up by text (R36) |

## Customers

| Objection | Honest answer | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "Is this text a scam?" | The first reply says who SOS SMS is, that it is automated, how to reach a person, the trip charge, and the guarantee scope, before any link. No link comes first | Decisions 3 and 9 |
| "What will it cost?" | The trip charge is $99, stated before anyone is sent, and credited to the repair if you go ahead, plus the $49 SOS SMS fee. The repair price comes from the contractor on site | Decisions 2 and 5 |
| "Are you the plumber?" | No. SOS SMS dispatches licensed, insured contractors. The contractor does the work | |
| "How fast will someone come?" | When a contractor accepts, you get their name and arrival time. If they are not there within 90 minutes of your request, the $99 trip charge comes back. Never an average | Decision 3 |
| "What if nobody accepts?" | We tell you within 15 minutes, and you pay nothing | Decision 3 |
| "Who sees my address?" | Only the contractor who takes your job. Others see the problem and the general area | Decision 4 |
| "Is the work guaranteed?" | SOS SMS checks license and insurance before a contractor can take a job. The work itself is the contractor's. No guarantee on the repair | |
| "I don't have a card on me." | A card or a phone wallet through the payment link is needed to send someone. Without one, the stop-the-water steps by text are free, and you pay nothing | R16 |
| "I have a home warranty." | If the failure is covered, the warranty is usually the cheaper route. Check it first | |
| "I'd rather talk to someone." | Text HELP and a person from SOS SMS replies by text within 15 minutes in covered hours, stated in the first reply | R50 (S72) |
| "I smell gas." | Get out first, then call 911 and your gas company. SOS SMS does not dispatch until it is safe | Decision 8 |

## Rules
Answer the objection that was asked, once, with the mechanic. Never answer with a number that is not in the brain, a testimonial, or "trust us". Every answer above uses decided values; never publish a softer guess.
