# Objection handling

Load for: FAQ pages, email replies, partner kit FAQs, the first reply, and any written answer to a pushback. Synthesized from both runs' Phase 5 blockers, Phase 7 key questions, the contractor page FAQ (Phase 8), and the cross-side check. There is no sales team: every answer is written, self-serve, and honest. Every answer carries the decided words (revised 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-21); contractor answers are written in the future tense before launch.

## Members, before joining

| Objection | Honest answer | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "Another subscription, for something that rarely happens?" / "Why pay before anything breaks?" | Yes, a membership costs something every month or year whether or not anything breaks. It buys readiness: your card and address on file, so a plumbing, HVAC, or electrical emergency is one text that reaches the vetted contractors near you, with an arrival time inside 90 minutes when one accepts. The trip charge only when you use it; cancel any time | R46, R53, R56 |
| "What does it cost?" | Basic $10 a month or $100 a year; Plus $15 or $150; Premier $35 or $350. Two months free on every annual plan. When you use it, the $99 trip charge goes on your card when a contractor accepts, and counts toward the repair. No fee per request. The repair price comes from the contractor on site | Decisions 2 and 5 |
| "Which plan do I need?" | Every plan gets the same one-text dispatch, at the same speed. Plus adds the 90-minute guarantee (6 months of membership credited on a late arrival or a no-show) and up to 5 phones in the home. Premier adds a 12-month credit, a trip charge a year paid by SOS SMS, a second home, and up to 10 phones. Basic covers your phone, with no credit. Upgrade any time | R53, R54, R65, R66, R69 |
| "How is this different from my warranty?" | A warranty pays for a covered repair, after a claim. SOS SMS gets a vetted contractor to your door with one text, with an arrival time. For a covered failure, the warranty is usually the cheaper route for the repair; many members hold both | R21 |
| "My plumber's club (or HVAC club) already gives me priority." | Priority with one company, for one or two trades. One text to SOS SMS reaches every vetted contractor on SOS SMS for your trade at once, in plumbing, HVAC, or electrical, with a window from your text | S75 |
| "Is my home covered at night?" | The ZIP check says whether memberships are open where you live. In an open zone, any hour, anywhere in `{{zone neighborhoods}}`. If your zone is not open yet, the launch list: no charge, no service promised | R15, R55 |
| "Can I cancel?" | Any time, on the membership page, or by texting HELP to reach a person. Annual members get a reminder before `{{renewal date}}` | R56 |
| "When does it start?" | On an annual plan, dispatch starts the day you join. On a monthly plan, 30 days after you join | R54 |
| "Why do you need my card now?" | So the emergency is one text: nothing to type or pay for at the worst moment. The card is charged for your plan, and the trip charge only when a contractor accepts. Add a backup card if you like | Decision 6, R16 |
| "Are you the plumber, or a warranty?" | Neither. SOS SMS is a membership that dispatches licensed, insured contractors. The contractor does the work; SOS SMS pays for no repair | R21 |

## Members, in the emergency

| Objection | Honest answer | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "How fast will someone come?" | When a contractor accepts, you get their name and an arrival time inside 90 minutes of your text. Never an average | Decision 3 |
| "What if they're late?" | The trip charge stands, because it pays for the work. On Plus and Premier, we credit the membership you paid in the 6 months (12 on Premier) before your request to your next bills, automatically. If no one is there after 90 minutes, text NEXT: the trip charge comes back in full and your request goes to the other contractors. On Basic, no credit | Decision 3, R17, R65, R66 |
| "What if nobody accepts?" | We tell you within 15 minutes, and you pay nothing | Decision 3, R11 |
| "Who sees my address?" | Only the contractor who takes your job. Others see the problem and the general area | Decision 4 |

| "I'd rather talk to someone." | Text HELP and a person from SOS SMS replies by text within 15 minutes, at any hour, in an open zone; the first reply says so | R50 (S72) |

## Rules
Answer the objection that was asked, once, with the mechanic. Never answer with a number that is not in the brain, a testimonial, or "trust us". Every answer above uses decided values; never publish a softer guess.
