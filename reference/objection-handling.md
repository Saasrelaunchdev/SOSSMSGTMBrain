# Objection handling

Load for: FAQ pages, email replies, partner kit FAQs, the first reply, and any written answer to a pushback. Synthesized from both runs' Phase 5 blockers, Phase 7 key questions, the contractor page FAQ (Phase 8), and the cross-side check. There is no sales team: every answer is written, self-serve, and honest. Brackets stay until their decision is logged.

## Contractors

| Objection | Honest answer | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "What's the catch? How do you make money?" | SOS SMS members pay a monthly or annual membership. You pay nothing in the founding period, then $25 per completed paid job, taken from the trip charge payout. No subscription, no per-lead fee | Decision 2 |
| "Every request is shared, like the platforms I quit." | It is shared, openly. Every matched contractor sees it at once and the first to accept gets it. Losing one costs nothing, because nobody pays to look | Decision 4 |
| "I've been burned by lead platforms." | Then read the terms before anything else: what you pay, when you're paid, what you commit to, what happens if you're late, on one page | The terms on one page |
| "When do I actually get the trip charge?" | It goes on the member's card when you accept, before you roll; SOS SMS holds it and releases it when you arrive. It reaches your bank within 2 business days | Decisions 3 and 6 |
| "What if I'm late?" | The trip charge is yours when you arrive by the time you committed to. Miss your own time and it goes back to the member. If SOS SMS caused the miss, SOS SMS pays the refund | Decision 3 |
| "Can I set my own trip charge?" | Your price for the work stays yours. The trip charge is one per trade and zone, set by SOS SMS: $99 for plumbing | Decision 5 |
| "I don't want calls at 2 a.m." | You mark the hours you're available. Decline anything, any time; declining doesn't change which requests reach you | Dispatch never ranks by acceptance |
| "My dispatcher runs the board." (shop) | You choose who receives requests: the dispatcher, the owner, or on-call technicians. Set it before the first request | Add-a-technician flow |
| "Will you steal my customers?" | The customer relationship stays yours. Pointing your own customers to SOS SMS membership for after-hours cover waits until dispatch gives you first refusal on them | R13 |
| "What if the member disputes?" | Text us. A founder settles it from the request log: the member's request text, your acceptance, your committed arrival time, and your arrival. The rule is in the FAQ before you join | Speed-to-scale plan, operations |
| "Is anyone in my area on it?" | Founding contractors are the first in {{zone}}. Memberships open there only when enough contractors have joined to cover it, and the zone's launch list grows meanwhile | Never a count |
| "Another app?" | Nothing to download. Requests arrive by text | Sign-up by text (R36) |

## Members, before joining

| Objection | Honest answer | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "Another subscription, for something that rarely happens?" | Yes, a membership costs something every month or year whether or not anything breaks. It buys one text that reaches the vetted contractors near you, an arrival time, and the 90-minute guarantee; the trip charge only when you use it; and you can cancel any time | R46, R53, R56 |
| "What does it cost?" | `{{monthly price}}` a month or `{{annual price}}` a year. When you use it, the $99 trip charge goes on your card when a contractor accepts, and counts toward the repair. No fee per request. The repair price comes from the contractor on site | Decisions 2 and 5 |
| "How is this different from my warranty?" | A warranty pays for a covered repair, after a claim. SOS SMS gets a vetted contractor to your door with one text, with an arrival time. For a covered failure, the warranty is usually the cheaper route for the repair; many members hold both | R21 |
| "My plumber's club already gives me priority." | Priority with one company. One text to SOS SMS reaches every vetted contractor on SOS SMS for your trade at once, with a window from your text | S75 |
| "Is my home covered at night?" | The ZIP check says whether memberships are open where you live, and the membership page names the covered hours. If your zone is not open yet, the launch list: no charge, no service promised | R55 |
| "Can I cancel?" | Any time, on the membership page, or by texting HELP to reach a person. Annual members get a reminder before `{{renewal date}}` | R56 |
| "Why do you need my card now?" | So the emergency is one text: nothing to type or pay for at the worst moment. The card is charged the membership, and the trip charge only when a contractor accepts | Decision 6 |
| "Are you the plumber, or a warranty?" | Neither. SOS SMS is a membership that dispatches licensed, insured contractors. The contractor does the work; SOS SMS pays for no repair | R21 |

## Members, in the emergency

| Objection | Honest answer | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "How fast will someone come?" | When a contractor accepts, you get their name and arrival time. If they are not there within 90 minutes of your text, the $99 trip charge comes back. Never an average | Decision 3 |
| "What if nobody accepts?" | We tell you within 15 minutes, and you pay nothing | Decision 3 |
| "Who sees my address?" | Only the contractor who takes your job. Others see the problem and the general area | Decision 4 |
| "Is the work guaranteed?" | SOS SMS checks license and insurance before a contractor can take a job. The work itself is the contractor's. No guarantee on the repair | R21 |
| "I'd rather talk to someone." | Text HELP and a person from SOS SMS replies by text within 15 minutes in covered hours, stated in the first reply | R50 (S72) |
| "I'm not a member, can you still send someone?" | Dispatch is for members. Here are the safety line and the steps to stop the water now, and how to join for next time | R57 |
| "I smell gas." | Get out first, then call 911 and your gas company. SOS SMS does not dispatch until it is safe | Decision 8 |

## Rules
Answer the objection that was asked, once, with the mechanic. Never answer with a number that is not in the brain, a testimonial, or "trust us". Every answer above uses decided values; never publish a softer guess.
