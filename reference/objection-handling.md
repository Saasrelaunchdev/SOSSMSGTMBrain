# Objection handling

Load for: FAQ pages, email replies, partner kit FAQs, the first reply, and any written answer to a pushback. Synthesized from both runs' Phase 5 blockers, Phase 7 key questions, the contractor page FAQ (Phase 8), and the cross-side check. There is no sales team: every answer is written, self-serve, and honest. Brackets stay until their decision is logged.

## Rules
Answer the objection that was asked, once, with the mechanic. Never answer with a number that is not in the brain, a testimonial, or "trust us". If the honest answer is a bracket, the FAQ entry waits; do not publish a softer guess.
