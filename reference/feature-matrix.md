# Feature matrix: what leads, what is table stakes

Load for: choosing what an asset leads with, and checking whether a feature can headline. Distills both runs' Phase 1 (`phase-1-product-feature-matrix.md`; the member side revised by the membership revision, 2026-10-11, and the tiers revision, 2026-10-20). Scores run -20 to +20 (demand / competitiveness), starting from Bryan's ratings in the source matrix and moved by research. Status of every item: `rules/feature-status.md`. Nothing is shipped.

Packaging (pricing model): the guarantee and more than one phone on file are Plus and Premier's; a second home and a trip charge a year paid by SOS SMS are Premier's; every other row is on every plan. Faster dispatch on a higher plan, paid priority, and repair coverage are rejected.

## Demand drivers
- **Contractors: the Lead Cost Check.** Last month's lead spend, leads, booked jobs, unpaid trips; returns true cost per booked job. Illustrative: a $40 lead that closes one time in six is a $240 job before the truck leaves (S03, S06). Variants: the automated lead spend report (replaces a white-glove audit), the Job Leak Scorecard, the emergency rate card template, a contractor test week on the standard terms (after launch).
- **Members: the readiness check, before anything breaks.** "How Ready Is Your Home for a 2 a.m. Leak?": seven questions, a score, and a plan, on the web or by texting READY; carried by affiliates and email; it ends at the ZIP check (join in an open zone, the launch list in a zone not yet open, the plan alone outside). Variants: the partner email version, a prevention guide with the check built in, Text READY, the welcome texts. The first-steps reply stays in the product, for members in an emergency and for any non-member who texts (R57); it is no longer a conversion moment.
