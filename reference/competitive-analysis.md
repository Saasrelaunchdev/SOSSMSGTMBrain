## Who each side weighs SOS SMS against

**Contractors:** C1 Angi Leads, C2 Thumbtack, C3 Google Local Services Ads, C4 Service Direct, C5 home warranty networks (American Home Shield, Frontdoor), C6 the status quo (word of mouth and their own listing). Answering services are a cost, not a source of jobs, so they appear only as a pricing anchor. Dropped: Jiffy and Puls (revisit when the metro is set); eLocal, Networx, Porch, Yelp, Bark (same model, less share of mind).

**Customers:** D1 search and call down the list, D2 Roto-Rooter, D3 Benjamin Franklin Plumbing, D4 Angi, D5 Thumbtack, D6 American Home Shield.

## What the reviews say
- **Contractors praise** volume, reach, exclusivity (C4), and budget control (C3, C4). **They criticize** paying for leads that never reply, the same request sent to several pros (C1, C2), unsubstantiated quality claims (C1, S01), warranty rates and price control (C5), and answering the phone themselves at night (C6).
- **Customers praise** availability (D2), punctuality guarantees (D3), purchase guarantees (D4, D5), fast replies (D5), and low cost for covered repairs (D6). **They criticize** unanswered calls and no-shows (D1), surprise after-hours fees (D1), upselling (D2, D3), floods of calls (D4), several pros contacting them (D5), and slow dispatch (D6).

## The scenario each side creates
- **Contractors:** SOS SMS is the obvious choice for contractors in the launch zone who already pay per lead, take emergency calls, and want to stop paying for requests that never become paid trips. It is not the choice for someone who wants exclusive calls or maximum volume. Do not pretend otherwise.
- **Customers:** the obvious choice for homeowners inside a dense founding zone with no one answering at night, who want one message and one committed arrival time. Not for someone who wants a known brand or a guarantee on the work.

## The wedge
Every lead platform earns when a request arrives, so none can stop charging to look without changing its model, and none handles payment for the visit. A single plumbing company cannot broadcast to rivals. A quote site earns from everyone calling back. Defensibility is local density, not technology: a rival needs the same contractors in a zone before it can make the same window promise.

## Do not contest
Volume and exclusivity (contractors). Brand familiarity and guarantees on the work (customers). SOS SMS checks licenses and insurance, not workmanship, and says so.
