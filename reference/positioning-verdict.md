# Positioning verdict: the forks already settled

Load for: any request that would reopen a strategic choice. Distills both run logs, both GTM briefs, the product-led revision (2026-10-01), and the marketplace synthesis. Dated entries live in `ops/decisions.md`; this file holds the reasoning.

## Fork 1. One GTM for the marketplace, or two
**Settled: two runs, never blended.** A contractor and a homeowner want opposite things from the same mechanic: the contractor wants the trip paid before rolling, the customer wants the trip charge stated before anyone is sent. One value proposition for both is true of neither. Each side ran the full nine phases; the cross-side check is what joins them.

## Fork 2. Which side first
**Settled: supply leads, demand follows, zone by zone.** Every customer promise (one text that reaches contractors, the arrival window, the guarantee) is only true where enough contractors have joined. Opening demand first would buy requests nobody can reach in time, and the guarantee could not launch.

## Fork 3. Sales-led or product-led
**Settled 2026-10-01: product-led, no sales team.** The founders changed the motion after the first runs. No calls, demos, meetings, shop visits, or audits on either side. Contractors arrive by cold email and partners and sign themselves up by text; customers arrive through bottom-of-funnel search and problem pages, and the product converts and retains them. The white-glove audit became the automated lead spend report.

## Fork 4. Where to fight
**Settled: fight where incumbents cannot follow.** Contractors: no charge for requests that never become work, and paid to show up. Customers: a guaranteed arrival window, and many contractors reached with one reply. Do not contest volume or exclusivity, or brand familiarity and guarantees on the work. The pitch is mechanics, not volume, which suits a company with no volume to show.

## Fork 5. Claims
**Settled: narrowed forms only.** Every "only" and "unique" in the source matrix failed either the rating rule or the research. Nothing invented, ever: pre-launch, the proof-point set is empty.

## Fork 6. Speed
**Settled: speed only inside proven zones.** Accept inefficiency in proving zones (paid search above break-even, $0 to founding contractors, generous refunds), never in claim accuracy, safety, payouts, refunds, privacy, or law. Slow down at thresholds set before launch.
