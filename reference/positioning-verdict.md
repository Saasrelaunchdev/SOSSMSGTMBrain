# Positioning verdict: the forks already settled

Load for: any request that would reopen a strategic choice. Distills both run logs, both GTM briefs, the product-led revision (2026-10-01), the membership revision (2026-10-11), and the marketplace synthesis. Dated entries live in `ops/decisions.md`; this file holds the reasoning.

## Fork 1. One GTM for the marketplace, or two
**Settled: two runs, never blended.** A contractor and a homeowner want opposite things from the same mechanic: the contractor wants the trip paid before rolling, the member wants the emergency to be one text, with the price settled long before. One value proposition for both is true of neither. Each side ran the full nine phases; the cross-side check is what joins them.

## Fork 2. Which side first
**Settled: supply leads, memberships follow, zone by zone.** Every member promise (one text that reaches contractors, the arrival window, the guarantee) is only true where enough contractors have joined. Selling memberships first would sell a promise nobody can keep, and the guarantee could not launch. Memberships open per zone at the density number; before then, the launch list (R55).

## Fork 3. Sales-led or product-led
**Settled 2026-10-01: product-led, no sales team.** The founders changed the motion after the first runs. No calls, demos, meetings, shop visits, or audits on either side. Contractors arrive by cold email and partners and sign themselves up by text. Since the membership revision (2026-10-11), members join before the need, reached through affiliates and email first, with search and SEO pages and paid sign-ups in support; the product converts and retains them. The white-glove audit became the automated lead spend report.

## Fork 4. Where to fight
**Settled: fight where incumbents cannot follow.** Contractors: no charge for requests that never become work, and paid to show up. Members: one text with a money-backed arrival window, and many contractors reached with one reply. Do not contest volume or exclusivity, or repair coverage, brand familiarity, and guarantees on the work. The pitch is mechanics, not volume, which suits a company with no volume to show.

## Fork 5. Claims
**Settled: narrowed forms only.** Every "only" and "unique" in the source matrix failed either the rating rule or the research. Nothing invented, ever: pre-launch, the proof-point set is empty.

## Fork 6. Speed
**Settled: speed only inside proven zones.** Accept inefficiency in proving zones (affiliate and sign-up spend up to the acquisition ceiling, $0 to founding contractors, generous refunds), never in claim accuracy, safety, payouts, refunds, privacy, or law. Slow down at thresholds set before launch.

## Fork 7. Pay per request or a membership
**Settled 2026-10-11, the membership revision: a membership, joined before the need.** A homeowner who has to search, check fit, sign up, and enter a card in the middle of an emergency is better served by calling a contractor from the results; a member whose card and address are already on file sends one text. Membership also opens the channels that only work before the need: affiliates and email. The contractor side did not change.
