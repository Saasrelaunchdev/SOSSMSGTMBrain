# Launch sequence and growth

Load for: what happens in what order, gates between stages, when to speed up or slow down, and what is never traded for speed. Distills `launch-sequence.md` and `speed-to-scale-plan.md` (product-led revision 2026-10-01; the membership revision 2026-10-11).

The organizing rule: **supply leads, memberships follow, zone by zone.** Member promises are only true where the contractor side has reached density, so memberships open only where they are true (R55). Before then, the zone's launch list grows: email, no charge, no service promised.

## Zone signals (planning thresholds, R32, set before launch so results cannot be argued after)
Acceptance inside the remaining window, 90 percent or more; arrival inside the window, 95 percent or more; refunds caused by SOS SMS, 5 or fewer per 100 requests; monthly plan churn, 6.5 percent or less (R59); cost per new paid member at or under the ceiling, about $43, by channel (R63); contractors accepting at least one job in 30 days, 60 percent or more. Sign-ups pause automatically when acceptance falls under 90 percent over 7 days: ads stop and affiliate links show the launch list (R34).

## Acceleration gates
1. **Zone proven:** every signal at threshold for 4 consecutive weeks (R33). Raise affiliate and sign-up spend while cost per new paid member holds under the ceiling and acceptance holds; extend covered hours.
2. **Zone full:** covered hours at the guarantee standard; members growing toward about 430 (R64). Open the adjacent zone from the template.
3. **Metro proven:** three zones pass without founders stepping in. Open a second metro.
4. **Trade added:** plumbing density holds and a referred trade reaches its own density; a second trade behind the same membership.

**Slow down** when refunds caused by SOS SMS, acceptance, monthly churn, cost per new paid member, or contractor activity cross their thresholds (decision 12: about $43 per new paid member, R63).

## Speed over efficiency
Accept: affiliate commissions and sign-up spend up to the ceiling in proving zones; $0 to founding contractors; over-recruiting for nights and freezes; generous automatic refunds; a text-only product; founders covering dispatch gaps by hand in the first zone only, each manual task with a date for software to replace it.

Never trade for speed: claim accuracy; safety triage before dispatch (S60); paying contractors on time; automatic refunds; easy cancellation (S87); privacy as promised; messaging, email, subscription, affiliate, and ad law (S31, S73, S86 to S88, S94, S70, S71).

## Ship before polished, but not before
Guarantee terms, safety triage, carrier registration, membership billing with easy cancellation, and payouts must work on day one. Leave for later: website polish, a second trade, scheduled maintenance, broad content.

## Unit economics, in one paragraph
At the planning price, a monthly member leaves about $8.51 a month of contribution in the founding period and $9.03 after it; the first 6 months of it, about $43, is the acquisition ceiling (R63). An affiliate at $20 (R61) fits under it; paid search at the plumbing benchmark, about $137 per new paid member (S15, B), does not, so it supports and never leads. Detail: `pricing-and-economics.md`.

## Stages of scale
A, under 10 people, one zone, founders do everything (a supply lead and a demand lead, R25); hire a partnerships and lifecycle email operator and a dispatch, payments, and billing engineer. B, tens, several zones: zone template, recruiting kit, member partnerships at metro scale, metric owners. C, hundreds, many metros: metro launch teams. Hire for the next stage, not forever.

## Risks
Acquisition costs more than a member is worth (pause the channel, shift to affiliates and the launch list, push annual plans, bring the contractor fee forward); members leave in quiet months (the readiness kit, seasonal notes, annual plans, honest renewal reminders, never a hard cancellation); dependence on one partner or one ad platform (several affiliates per zone, SOS SMS's own list, organic pages); too few requests for contractors (grow the launch list before opening, recruit to the density number); thin zones break the guarantee (narrow covered hours); incumbents add an arrival guarantee (density is the defense); contractors multi-home (payout speed, fair late-arrival rule).

## What would change the sequence
Memberships first: the guarantee cannot launch, and members would pay for a promise nobody can keep. A contractor fee from day one: contractor drills 1 to 3 change. No Google eligibility: affiliates and email carry sign-ups alone, with Microsoft and organic pages. Property managers: a third run.
