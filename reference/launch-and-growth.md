# Launch sequence and growth

Load for: what happens in what order, gates between stages, when to speed up or slow down, and what is never traded for speed. Distills `launch-sequence.md` and `speed-to-scale-plan.md` (product-led revision 2026-10-01; the membership revision 2026-10-11; the tiers revision 2026-10-20; the pricing revision 2026-10-21).

The organizing rule: **supply leads, memberships follow, zone by zone.** Member promises are only true where the contractor side has reached density, so memberships open only where they are true (R55). Before then, the zone's launch list grows: email, no charge, no service promised.

## Zone signals (planning thresholds, R32, set before launch so results cannot be argued after)
Acceptance inside the remaining window, 90 percent or more, in each trade; arrival inside the window, 95 percent or more; late arrivals and no-shows, 5 or fewer per 100 accepted requests; monthly plan churn, 6.5 percent or less (R59); cost per new paid member at or under the ceiling of the plan joined, about $47 Basic, $70 Plus, $151 Premier, by channel (R63); contractors accepting at least one job in 30 days, 60 percent or more, in plumbing and HVAC (over 6 months in electrical). Sign-ups pause automatically when acceptance in any trade falls under 90 percent over 7 days: paid social stops and affiliate links show the launch list (R34).

## Acceleration gates
1. **Zone proven:** every signal at threshold for 4 consecutive weeks (R33). Raise affiliate and paid social spend while cost per new paid member holds under the ceiling and acceptance holds in every trade and hour band.
2. **Zone full:** every trade and hour band holding the density number; members growing toward about 410 (R64). Open the adjacent zone from the template.
3. **Metro proven:** three zones pass without founders stepping in. Open a second metro.
4. **Trade added:** plumbing, HVAC, and electrical density hold and a referred trade (water damage, then locksmith) reaches its own density; a fourth trade behind the same membership.

**Slow down** when late arrivals and no-shows, acceptance, monthly churn, cost per new paid member against the ceiling of the plan joined (decision 12, R63), or contractor activity cross their thresholds.

## Speed over efficiency
Accept: affiliate commissions and paid social spend up to the ceiling in proving zones; $0 to join and per request for contractors; over-recruiting for nights, freezes, and heat waves; the late-arrival credit applied generously and automatically on Plus and Premier; a text-only product; founders covering dispatch gaps by hand in the first zone only, each manual task with a date for software to replace it.

Never trade for speed: claim accuracy; safety triage before dispatch (S60); paying contractors on time; crediting members automatically; easy cancellation (S87); privacy as promised; messaging, email, subscription, and affiliate law, and ad policy (S31, S73, S86 to S88, S94; S70, S71 should a brand campaign ever run).

## Ship before polished, but not before
The plan terms and the guarantee, safety triage, carrier registration, membership billing with easy cancellation, and payouts must work on day one. Leave for later: website polish, a fourth trade, non-emergency scheduling, broad content.

## Unit economics, in one paragraph
Illustrative, from the pricing model: a monthly member leaves about $9.29 a month of contribution on Basic, $13.77 on Plus, and $29.62 on Premier, about $14.91 at the plan mix; the first 6 months of it, about $47, $70, and $151, is each plan's acquisition ceiling (R63). An affiliate at $20 (R61) fits under every ceiling; paid search at the plumbing benchmark, about $137 per new paid member (S15, B), does not, so there is none at launch (decision 11). Detail: `pricing-and-economics.md`.

## Stages of scale
A, under 10 people, one zone in three trades, founders do everything (a supply lead recruiting in all three trades and a demand lead, R25); hire a partnerships and lifecycle email operator and a dispatch, payments, and billing engineer. B, tens, several zones: zone template, recruiting kit, member partnerships at metro scale, metric owners. C, hundreds, many metros: metro launch teams. Hire for the next stage, not forever.

## Risks
Acquisition costs more than a member is worth (pause the channel, shift to affiliates and the launch list, push Plus on the annual plan); members leave in quiet months (the readiness kit, seasonal notes, annual plans, honest renewal reminders, never a hard cancellation); dependence on one partner or one ad platform (several affiliates per zone, SOS SMS's own list, organic pages); too few requests for contractors, first in electrical (grow the launch list before opening, recruit to the density number, tell each contractor the expected volume for their trade); thin zones break the guarantee (sign-ups pause, R34; recruit for the thin trade and hour bands; requests keep going out, R15); incumbents add an arrival guarantee (density is the defense); contractors multi-home (payout speed, the trip charge kept on a late arrival, no charge for a request they do not win).

## What would change the sequence
Memberships first: the arrival time and the guarantee on Plus and Premier cannot launch, and members would pay for a promise nobody can keep. A zone opened with plumbing alone: members pay for three trades they cannot all use; sell it as plumbing only there and rewrite the membership page. Paid search at launch: Google's written confirmation per zone (S70, S71), and measured cost under the plan's ceiling, about 3 times lower than the benchmark (decision 11). Property managers: a third run.
