# Launch sequence and growth

Load for: what happens in what order, gates between stages, when to speed up or slow down, and what is never traded for speed. Distills `launch-sequence.md` and `speed-to-scale-plan.md` (both 2026-10-01, product-led revision).

The organizing rule: **supply leads, demand follows, zone by zone.** Customer promises are only true where the contractor side has reached density, and paid search only runs where they are true.

## Stages and gates

| Stage | Work | Gate to leave |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Decisions and plumbing | The twelve decisions (made 2026-10-03) written into the terms, the guarantee, and the founding agreement; Google eligibility in writing and message assets verified (S70 to S72); carrier registration (S32); payment hold, capture, release, payout tested; both self-serve paths tested end to end; terms and guarantee counsel-reviewed; zone thresholds (R32) in the weekly review; the founding zone drawn and counted (R40, R41); local competitor slate | All of it written down, tested, and reviewed |
| 1 Supply fills the founding zone | Zone contractor list and emails 1 to 4; one supply house and the local association chapter; sign-up by text; test requests by hour band | Density number met for the first trade, zone, and covered hours |
| 2 Demand opens through search | Google ad groups geofenced and scheduled; zone page and five problem guides; first reply, guarantee page, automatic refunds; product loops; Microsoft import | Zone signals tracked weekly |
| 3 Prove, then accelerate | Gates below | Zone proven |

## Zone signals (planning thresholds, R32, set before launch so results cannot be argued after)
Acceptance inside the remaining window, 90 percent or more; arrival inside the window, 95 percent or more; refunds caused by SOS SMS, 5 or fewer per 100 requests; first text to paid, 50 percent or more; owned share, 30 percent by the end of month 6 and 50 percent by month 12; contractors accepting at least one job in 30 days, 60 percent or more. Campaigns pause automatically when acceptance falls under 90 percent over 7 days (R34).

## Acceleration gates
1. **Zone proven:** every signal at threshold for 4 consecutive weeks (R33). Raise ad budget as far as acceptance holds; extend covered hours.
2. **Zone full:** covered hours at the guarantee standard, owned share rising. Open the adjacent zone from the template.
3. **Metro proven:** three zones pass without founders stepping in. Open a second metro.
4. **Trade added:** plumbing density holds and a referred trade reaches its own density.

**Slow down** when refunds caused by SOS SMS, acceptance, contractor activity, or cost per paid job cross their thresholds (the ceiling: $275 while proving, $192 by month 6, $137 by month 12, decision 12).

## Speed over efficiency
Accept: paid search above break-even in proving zones; $0 to founding contractors; over-recruiting for nights and freezes; generous automatic refunds; a text-only product; founders covering dispatch gaps by hand in the first zone only, each manual task with a date for software to replace it.

Never trade for speed: claim accuracy; safety triage before dispatch (S60); paying contractors on time; automatic refunds; privacy as promised; messaging, email, and ad law (S31, S73, S70 to S72).

## Ship before polished, but not before
Guarantee terms, safety triage, carrier registration, and payouts must work on day one. Leave for later: website polish, a second trade, membership, scheduled maintenance, broad content.

## Stages of scale
A, under 10 people, one zone, founders do everything (a supply lead and a demand lead, R25); hire a paid search operator and a dispatch and payments engineer. B, tens, several zones: zone template, recruiting kit, metric owners. C, hundreds, many metros: metro launch teams. Hire for the next stage, not forever.

## Risks
Paid search never pays back (slow spend, push owned loops, bring the contractor fee forward); dependence on Google (keep Microsoft and organic live); thin zones break the guarantee (narrow covered hours); incumbents add arrival guarantees (density is the defense); contractors multi-home (payout speed, fair late-arrival rule).

## What would change the sequence
Demand first: the guarantee cannot launch. A contractor fee from day one: contractor drills 1 to 3 change. No Google eligibility: lead with Microsoft and problem pages. Property managers: a third run.
