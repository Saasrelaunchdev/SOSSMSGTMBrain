# ICP and buying committee

Load for: who an asset is for, who blocks, what wins each level, and how to find them. Distills both runs' Phase 4 and Phase 5 (the member side revised by the membership revision, 2026-10-11). Every row applies inside the founding zone: a 10-mile radius around the founding contractors' shops in the founders' home metro (decision 1).

## Contractors

| | Level 1: word-of-mouth emergency taker | Level 2: already paying for jobs |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Owner-operator plumbers and water-emergency contractors living on referrals | Small plumbing and water damage shops, owner plus one to five technicians, with at least one paid lead source |
| Pain | Unpaid trips; night calls; slow weeks; no budget for lead platforms | Cost per booked job; shared leads; leads that never reply; after-hours dispatch overhead (S03 to S08) |
| Hypothesis | Overflow emergencies in hours they choose, costing nothing when quiet, with the trip paid before they roll | No per-lead cost; a request they do not win costs nothing; the trip charge collected before they roll |
| Triggers | Slow season; a run of unpaid trips; a new helper or truck | A lead platform renewal or price rise; a month of unreplied leads; a new technician; freeze season |
| Guarantee fit | Limited: one person cannot always roll at 2 a.m. | Good: an on-call technician can |

**Level 2 goes first:** it has a savings anchor (cost per booked job today), night capacity, and has already accepted platforms. **Level 1 is recruited alongside** to fill hours and refer other trades. **Disqualifiers:** unlicensed or uninsured; won't credit the trip charge; won't commit to an arrival time.

### Committee
- **Level 1:** the owner-operator is user, manager, and decision maker. Blocks on: burned by a platform, suspects a catch in "no fees", won't commit at 2 a.m., no more apps. Wins with: the terms on one page, how a job works in three texts, the Lead Cost Check.
- **Level 2:** technician (user), dispatcher (manager), owner (decision maker). **The dispatcher is the likely blocker**, because a request accepted on a technician's phone goes around the dispatch board. Settle who receives requests before the first one arrives. The owner blocks on platform distrust (S01), a race to the bottom, and liability for a missed arrival. Win the dispatcher with where an SOS SMS job enters their flow, a sample payout record, and the dispute FAQ.

### The founding cohort
Licensed, insured, takes emergency calls, spare capacity some nights, willing to credit the trip charge and commit to an arrival time, willing to give feedback. Sized by `density-model.md`. Found by cold email, supply house counters, the PHCC chapter (S24), and referrals.

## Members

| | Level 1: a new homeowner without a go-to plumber | Level 2: an established homeowner whose plumber does not answer at night |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Homeowner, often a recent buyer or new to the area (first-time buyers 24 percent of buyers in the 2024 profile, S91) | Established homeowner, usually in an older home; sometimes a small landlord with one or two rentals, one membership per address (R54) |
| Pain | Who to trust; who would come at night; where the shutoff is; what a fair call-out costs | Voicemail at 2 a.m.; unexpected after-hours fees; no-shows (S50, S51); a warranty claim that took days (S20) |
| Hypothesis | Ready before anything breaks: one number saved, the card and address on file, so the emergency is one text to vetted contractors, with an arrival time and the 90-minute guarantee | The text to send when their plumber does not answer: many vetted contractors at once, a money-backed window, and their own plumber kept for daytime work |
| Triggers | Moving in and setting up services; the first freeze; a neighbor's flood story; a home inspection that flagged old plumbing | An emergency where nobody answered; a warranty claim denied or slow; their plumber retiring or booked up; freeze season |
| Found through | Utility new-account offers, association welcome packets, the readiness scorecard, prevention guides, agents and inspectors who share SOS SMS without payment | Email from their association or insurance agent (existing policyholders), prevention guides, search at freeze season, a neighbor's forwarded card |

Both levels join before the need, through the partners and lists that already reach them, never through an ad at the moment of the emergency. Grow each zone's launch list during Stage 1; email it first when memberships open; let affiliates and email carry sign-ups, with search and social in support. Cost per new paid member by channel against the ceiling (R63).

### Committee
A household: one or two adults, deciding over days, at a kitchen table or in an inbox. **The blocker is "another subscription"**: a fee every month or year before anything breaks (R46). Others: "it rarely happens"; "is my home covered at night"; "what does it cost when I use it"; "how is this different from my warranty"; a card on file with a company they do not know yet; "can I cancel". Wins with: the price and the trip charge on use stated plainly; the guarantee with its scope; cancel any time; the readiness scorecard; a trusted partner's mention; the privacy line. Level 2 also needs to hear they keep their usual contractor for daytime work, and that a warranty pays for repairs while SOS SMS gets someone there.

### Scope
The founding member area is the neighborhoods inside the founding contractor coverage, nowhere else, and memberships open there only at the density number (R55). Renters are out (the landlord is a second decision maker). Life-safety emergencies go to 911 and the utility first, member or not (S60). Property managers would be a third run. About 430 members per zone keep each of 9 founding contractors at about one request a month (planning value, R64).

## Permission rules (the only path to proof)
Contractors: a permission clause before the first job. Members: a clause in the member terms and a two-question follow-up text (arrived within the window? tell a neighbor?), recorded verbatim with the date. A quote enters `ops/copy-bank.md` only with permission on record.
