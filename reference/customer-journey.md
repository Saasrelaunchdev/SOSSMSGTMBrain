# Customer journeys, both sides

Load for: writing to a funnel stage, choosing the touchpoint, and the KPI each asset is judged by. Distills both runs' Phase 7 (`phase-7-customer-journey.md`, product-led revision 2026-10-01; the member side revised by the membership revision, 2026-10-11). Self-serve throughout: no calls, visits, or meetings. At launch the founders cover every function marked Product.

## Contractor journey: weeks, self-serve

| Stage | Mindset | Touchpoints | KPI | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Awareness | "Leads that never called back, and two trips I never got paid for" | Cold email 1 with the Lead Cost Check; counter card QR; partner newsletter; a forwarded check | Lead Cost Checks completed, by channel | Knows their real cost per booked job |
| 2 Discovery | "What's the catch?" | Email 2, how a job works in three texts; contractor page and FAQ; partner explainer video | Page visits; FAQ views; replies by objection | Understands mechanics and obligations, including the shared request |
| 3 Evaluation | "Show me the terms, not the pitch" | Email 3, the terms on one page; the automated lead spend report; payout and dispute FAQ | Terms views to JOIN | Decides to try, without talking to anyone |
| 4 Trial | "Sign me up. How long until I'm live?" | JOIN by text; license and insurance upload; payout setup; agreement with the permission clause; a test request | Time from JOIN to verified; drop-off step | Verified, payout-ready, available |
| 5 Value | "Did the trip charge actually show up?" | First-request walkthrough text; payout confirmation; the fifth-job three-question text | Days to first paid trip; share paid before rolling | First paid trip, money received |
| 6 Advocacy | "The water damage guy I work with should be on this" | Fifth-trip referral prompt; add a technician; hours and area by text | Referrals per active contractor | Brings in other trades on their own |
| 7 Staying active | "Still worth keeping my phone on?" | Monthly text summary; 30-day no-acceptance check-in | Active contractors vs density target; acceptance by hour band | Stays available; the zone keeps its density |

Critical: every term in writing before sign-up; a fast first paid trip; payouts on time, every time; density tracked weekly. Blockers: onboarding friction (verification, payout checks); payout timing unbuilt; first-job silence (recruit by zone, and grow the zone's launch list in parallel so memberships open with members waiting); the late-arrival rule. Accelerators: supply house counters; cross-trade referral at the five-job check-in, not before; send the founding offer first to the heaviest lead spenders; launch one zone, not one metro.

## Member journey: two clocks, weeks before the need and minutes during it

| Stage | Mindset | Touchpoints | KPI | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Awareness | "Who would I even call at 2 a.m.?" Set off by moving in, the first freeze, a neighbor's flood story, or a night when nobody answered | An affiliate's mention (utility, association, newsletter, insurance agent); a partner's email or SOS SMS's own; the readiness scorecard; prevention guides | Scorecards completed; email sign-ups and affiliate clicks by channel | Knows the gap in the home's readiness, and that one number fills it |
| 2 Discovery | "What is it, and is my home covered?" | The membership page with the ZIP check; how it works in one text; the launch list for zones not yet open; sign-up ads in open zones | ZIP checks by channel; launch list joins by zone | Knows whether the home is covered; on the launch list if the zone is not open |
| 3 Evaluation | "Is it worth it? What does it cost when I use it?" | **The membership page**: `{{monthly price}}` or `{{annual price}}`; the $99 trip charge on use, credited to the repair; the guarantee in full with its scope; what is not included; cancel any time; privacy | Membership page to join rate; drop-off by section | Decides to join with the full picture |
| 4 Join | "Sign me up" | Card, home address, consent to the terms (R54); the welcome texts; the contact card; the readiness kit | Cost per new paid member by channel, against the ceiling (R63); time to join | A member, with nothing left to set up |
| 5 Value | "Something broke. One text." Weeks or months later | **The first reply**, at once: going to the contractors now, the safety line, how to reach a person; the stop-the-water steps; the acceptance text with name, arrival time, rating, and the $99 trip charge on the card on file; arrival; the refund if late | Time from the request text to acceptance; share inside the window; refund rate | Promise kept, or the refund paid without a fight |
| 6 Advocacy | "My neighbor should have this" | Two-question follow-up; the forwardable card; the permission request | Referrals per member; quotes with permission | Recommends; proof captured with permission |
| 7 Renewal | "Still worth keeping?" Most months nothing breaks | Seasonal notes by email, by text only with opt-in (S31, S73); the renewal reminder before `{{renewal date}}`; cancel any time | Monthly churn against 6.5 percent (R59); annual renewal against 75 percent (R60) | Renews, number still saved; the next emergency starts at stage 5 |

Critical: memberships open only where supply is ready (R55); the membership page decides; the first reply decides the emergency; refunds automatic and unasked; a membership worth keeping in the quiet months. Blockers: another subscription (R46); is the home covered at night; can the text get through (S32); what if no one accepts (decision 3); a card that fails, caught at billing before an emergency finds it. Accelerators: affiliates that reach a whole zone at once (S93); the launch list; the first freeze, with guides and the scorecard before it; Microsoft Ads for sign-ups (S74, C). The source's "founding contractors' own customers" accelerator (pointing them to SOS SMS membership for after-hours cover) is blocked by conflict E until first refusal exists.

## The hand-off
Acceptance is where both journeys meet: the contractor's commitment becomes the member's arrival time, inside the remaining window, or there is no acceptance at all. Shared KPIs: time from the request text to acceptance; share arriving within the window; share of refunds caused by SOS SMS. Value realization is the first paid trip for the contractor and arrival inside the window for the member. Both rest on infrastructure: payouts, carrier registration, membership billing, the card on file.
