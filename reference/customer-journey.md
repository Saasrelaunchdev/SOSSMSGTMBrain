# Customer journeys, both sides

Load for: writing to a funnel stage, choosing the touchpoint, and the KPI each asset is judged by. Distills both runs' Phase 7 (`phase-7-customer-journey.md`, product-led revision 2026-10-01). Self-serve throughout: no calls, visits, or meetings. At launch the founders cover every function marked Product.

## Contractor journey: weeks, self-serve

| Stage | Mindset | Touchpoints | KPI | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Awareness | "Leads that never called back, and two trips I never got paid for" | Cold email 1 with the Lead Cost Check; counter card QR; partner newsletter; a forwarded check | Lead Cost Checks completed, by channel | Knows their real cost per booked job |
| 2 Discovery | "What's the catch?" | Email 2, how a job works in three texts; contractor page and FAQ; partner explainer video | Page visits; FAQ views; replies by objection | Understands mechanics and obligations, including the shared request |
| 3 Evaluation | "Show me the terms, not the pitch" | Email 3, the terms on one page; the automated lead spend report; payout and dispute FAQ | Terms views to JOIN | Decides to try, without talking to anyone |
| 4 Trial | "Sign me up. How long until I'm live?" | JOIN by text; license and insurance upload; payout setup; agreement with the permission clause; a test request | Time from JOIN to verified; drop-off step | Verified, payout-ready, available |
| 5 Value | "Did the trip charge actually show up?" | First-request walkthrough text; payout confirmation; the fifth-job three-question text | Days to first paid trip; share paid before rolling | First paid trip, money received |
| 6 Advocacy | "The water damage guy I work with should be on this" | Fifth-trip referral prompt; add a technician; hours and area by text | Referrals per active contractor | Brings in other trades on their own |
| 7 Staying active | "Still worth keeping my phone on?" | Monthly text summary; 30-day no-acceptance check-in | Active contractors vs density target; acceptance by hour band | Stays available; the zone keeps its density |

Critical: every term in writing before sign-up; a fast first paid trip; payouts on time, every time; density tracked weekly. Blockers: onboarding friction (verification, payout checks); payout timing unbuilt; first-job silence (recruit by zone); the late-arrival rule. Accelerators: supply house counters; cross-trade referral at the five-job check-in, not before; send the founding offer first to the heaviest lead spenders; launch one zone, not one metro.

## Customer journey: minutes

| Stage | Mindset | Touchpoints | KPI | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Awareness | "Water is coming through the ceiling and it's 1 a.m." | Problem ad groups and guides, stop-the-water first; prevention guides and Text READY | Text-button taps; READY texts | Knows what to do first, and that one text gets help |
| 2 Discovery | "Who can I get at this hour?" | Emergency ads with text-from-ad; zone page; saved contact card; a forwarded card | First texts by source; cost per first text; owned share | Sends the first text |
| 3 Evaluation | "Is this legit? What will it cost?" | **The first reply**: who SOS SMS is, what happens next, the trip charge, the guarantee scope, all before any link; the safety line | Reply-to-agreement rate | Agrees with the full picture |
| 4 Purchase | "OK, send someone" | Payment link after agreement; acceptance text with name, arrival time, rating | Time to acceptance; share with no acceptance in time | Paid, with a named contractor and a time |
| 5 Value | "They're here, inside the window" | Arrival text; if late, the guarantee applied automatically; receipt showing the credit | Share inside the window; refund rate | Promise kept, or refund paid without a fight |
| 6 Advocacy | "My neighbor had the same thing last winter" | Two-question follow-up; forwardable card; permission request | Forwards per job; quotes with permission | Recommends; proof captured with permission |
| 7 Loyalty | "If this happens again, I know who to text" | Contact card kept; seasonal texts only with opt-in (S31) | Saved-number rate; repeat requests | Next emergency starts at stage 3 |

Critical: the first reply decides everything; density before demand; refunds automatic and unasked; the owned share. Blockers: can the buyer pay (no card, S61); does the link look like a scam (S59); can the text get through (S32); what if no one accepts (decision 3). Accelerators: problem keywords first; Microsoft Ads import (S74, C); raise bids at the first freeze only where density holds. The source's "founding contractors' own customers" accelerator is blocked by conflict E until first refusal exists.

## The hand-off
Acceptance is where both journeys meet: the contractor's commitment becomes the customer's arrival time, inside the remaining window, or there is no acceptance at all. Shared KPIs: time to acceptance; share arriving within the window; share of refunds caused by SOS SMS. Value realization is the first paid trip for the contractor and arrival inside the window for the customer. Both rest on infrastructure: payouts, carrier registration, link trust, card availability.
