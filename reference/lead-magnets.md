## Contractors: the Job Leak Scorecard
"How much of your work leaks away before you get paid?" Seven questions, 0 to 100, higher means more leakage, under three minutes. Built from the Lead Cost Check.

| # | Question | Reveals |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly spend on lead platforms or search ads | Exposure to per-lead cost |
| 2 | Share of paid leads that become booked jobs ("I don't buy leads" routes Level 1) | Close rate |
| 3 | Unpaid trips last month | The leak the trip charge closes |
| 4 | When the trip fee is collected | Exposure to unpaid trips |
| 5 | Who answers after 6 p.m. | After-hours coverage |
| 6 | Emergency calls missed or turned away a week | Uncaptured demand |
| 7 | Nights a week they would take paid-in-advance requests | Fit and capacity |

Tiers: 0 to 39 Tight Ship (soft: rate card template); 40 to 69 Leaking at the Edges (read the terms on one page, run the Lead Cost Check); 70 to 100 Paying to Work (the automated lead spend report, then text JOIN to {{SOS SMS number}}). The result shows their own cost per booked job, labeled "estimate from your answers". Emails on days 0, 2, 5, 8 (day 8: what every contractor gets and commits to, and that the zone opens to members once every hour is covered). No urgency and no deadline: every contractor joins on the same terms whenever they join (R07, retired). Texts only with a separate opt-in (S31).

## Members: How Ready Is Your Home for a 2 a.m. Leak?
The Home Emergency Readiness Scorecard, also run as Text READY. Seven questions, under two minutes. The top of SOS SMS's own email list, the first thing a partner shares (in the affiliate kit), and the offer on every prevention guide (the membership revision, 2026-10-11). It leads with leaks, the most feared emergency (49 percent of homeowners, S82); its plan and the membership cover plumbing, HVAC, and electrical (the tiers revision, 2026-10-20).

| # | Question | Reveals |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where the main water shutoff is | Whether a leak can be stopped |
| 2 | When the shutoff was last tested | Whether it will turn |
| 3 | Water heater age | The largest water store |
| 4 | Home age (median owner-occupied home is 42 years, S63) | Older plumbing, heating, and wiring |
| 5 | A plumber, HVAC company, and electrician who answer at night | The gap SOS SMS fills, in three trades |
| 6 | First move if a pipe bursts at 2 a.m. | Whether they have a plan |
| 7 | A number saved for home emergencies | The step membership answers |

Tiers: Ready (soft: see what membership includes), Partly Ready (the shutoff guide, then the ZIP check), Exposed (check your ZIP and join before you need it). The ZIP decides the ending: an open zone shows the membership (Plus at $150 a year shown first; plans from $100 a year or $10 a month); a zone not yet open, the launch list; outside, the plan alone. The results page carries the safety line. Emails on days 0 (the score and the plan), 2 (the first ten minutes of a leak, EPA 24 to 48 hour drying, S58), 5 (how membership works in one text, for plumbing, HVAC, or electrical; the three plans and the $99 trip charge on use; the guarantee with its plans and scope; what SOS SMS does not do), and 8 (seasonal only when true; otherwise "Ready before it happens?"). Every email carries the full name, a postal address, and an opt-out (S73). No damage-cost figures without a source; no claim that SOS SMS prevents damage.
