# Lead magnets

Load for: building, editing, or writing follow-up for either scorecard. Distills both runs' `lead-magnet-spec.md`. Neither uses case studies or projections, because none exist.

## Contractors: the Job Leak Scorecard
"How much of your work leaks away before you get paid?" Seven questions, 0 to 100, higher means more leakage, under three minutes. Built from the Lead Cost Check.

| # | Question | Reveals |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly spend on lead platforms or search ads | Exposure to per-lead cost |
| 2 | Share of paid leads that become booked jobs ("I don't buy leads" routes Level 1) | Close rate |
| 3 | Unpaid trips last month | The leak the trip charge closes |
| 4 | When the trip fee is collected | Exposure to unpaid trips |
| 5 | Who answers after 6 p.m. | After-hours coverage |
| 6 | Emergency calls missed or turned away a week | Uncaptured demand |
| 7 | Nights a week they would take paid-in-advance requests | Fit and capacity |

Tiers: 0 to 39 Tight Ship (soft: rate card template); 40 to 69 Leaking at the Edges (read the terms on one page, run the Lead Cost Check); 70 to 100 Paying to Work (the automated lead spend report, then text JOIN to {{SOS SMS number}}). The result shows their own cost per booked job, labeled "estimate from your answers". Emails on days 0, 2, 5, 8; the only deadline is the real founding list close date, {{founding close date}} (R07). Texts only with a separate opt-in (S31).

## Customers: How Ready Is Your Home for a 2 a.m. Leak?
The Home Emergency Readiness Scorecard, also run as Text READY. Seven questions, under two minutes.

| # | Question | Reveals |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where the main water shutoff is | Whether a leak can be stopped |
| 2 | When the shutoff was last tested | Whether it will turn |
| 3 | Water heater age | The largest water store |
| 4 | Home age (median owner-occupied home is 42 years, S63) | Older plumbing |
| 5 | A plumber who answers at night | The gap SOS SMS fills |
| 6 | First move if a pipe bursts at 2 a.m. | Whether they have a plan |
| 7 | A number saved for home emergencies | The conversion step |

Tiers: Ready, Partly Ready, Exposed, each ending in text READY. The results page carries the safety line. ZIP codes outside the founding zone get the plan without the SOS SMS offer. Emails on days 0, 2 (the first ten minutes of a leak, EPA 24 to 48 hour drying, S58), 5, and 8 (seasonal only when true). No damage-cost figures without a source; no claim that SOS SMS prevents damage.
